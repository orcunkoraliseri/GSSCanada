# RT02. The ten candidate angles: gap check, landscape, and ranking

## Section A. Direct answer

Of the ten candidate angles evaluated, two angles (A1 and A10) are substantially taken in their core deliverable by recent publications, six angles (A2, A4, A5, A6, A8, and A9) are partly taken in their component methods but remain open in their specific combination, and two angles (A3 and A7) are unclaimed. Specifically, A1 (Agentic UBEM) has been claimed at cohort scale by Wu et al. (2026, SSRN 7333555) with Geo2UBEM, which deployed an LLM multi-agent system for automated scan-to-IDF geometry abstraction and EnergyPlus calibration without metered data, while Shirzadi et al. (2026, Energy and AI 100896) benchmarked autonomous versus tool-augmented LLM agents modifying Canadian NECB EnergyPlus models. Similarly, A10 (Reference bands for stacked mixed-use buildings) has been directly addressed by Lim and Koo (2026, SSRN 7083529), who built activity-level peer-group EUI benchmarking across 3,001 mixed-use buildings. In contrast, A3 (explaining why an LLM occupancy generator loses to a raked donor pool across countries) and A7 (an LLM reading building records with abstention feeding a conformally bounded UBEM) have no matching precedent in the literature. Ranking all candidate angles under an explicit rule that penalises crowded fields and prioritises verified asset fit places A7 first, followed by A9 and A2, while A10 should be dropped immediately as a narrow duplicate.

## Section E. What this changes in our planning

### 1. Ranking of candidate angles

#### Ranking rule
The ranking is computed using an explicit score:
$$\text{Score} = 3.0 \times \text{Openness} + 2.0 \times \text{AssetFit} - 1.5 \times \text{ObjectionVulnerability} - 1.0 \times \text{EffortNormalized}$$
where:
- Openness: Unclaimed (`yes` = 3.0), Partly taken (`partly` = 1.5), Taken (`no` = 0.0). Openness is weighted highest to prevent duplicating published work.
- AssetFit: High alignment with held corpora and OpenUBEM engine (3.0), Medium (2.0), Low (1.0).
- ObjectionVulnerability: Severe unanswerable objection (3.0), Moderate answerable objection (2.0), Low objection (1.0).
- EffortNormalized: Effort in months divided by 3 (range 1.33 to 3.33).

#### Angle to drop first
Angle A10 must be dropped first. As shown by Lim and Koo (2026, Section C row 30), building-level standardized scoring across activity peer groups using floor-area weighting has already been developed and demonstrated on 3,001 buildings. Proceeding with A10 would produce an incremental paper in an already occupied niche. Similarly, A1 is crowded by Geo2UBEM (Wu et al. 2026, Section C row 1) and MCP agent workflows (Shirzadi et al. 2026, Section C row 2).
