# RT12: Contradictions and ranking of the surviving angles

## Section A. Direct answer

Passive survivability under power failure with demographically resolved occupants (A9) ranks first alongside the privacy-utility and release protocol (A12), while occupancy under heat (A2), occupancy-resolved energy burden (A6), and records reading with abstention (A7) form the immediate second tier [INFERENCE].
The surviving open angles rank in order: A9 (S = 27.0), A12 (S = 27.0), A2 (S = 24.5), A6 (S = 24.5), A7 (S = 24.5), A4 (S = 24.0), A11 (S = 19.0), A3 (S = 17.0), A8 (S = 16.5), A13 (S = 16.0), and A14 in activity-based travel model form (S = 16.0) [INFERENCE].
The Part A verdicts resolve several previously open contradictions across the candidate set [INFERENCE].
First, pyepwmorph is at version 2.2.0 under an MIT licence at justinfmccarty/pyepwmorph, whereas the alternative repository returns 404 [L1, L2, L3].
Second, IEA EBC Annex 79 concluded in 2024 and Annex 95 is ongoing from 2024 to 2029 titled Human-centric Building Design and Operation for a Changing Climate [L4, L5, L6].
Third, no published study of indoor conditions during a winter power outage in Canadian multi-unit residential buildings exists, and the claimed Baba et al. 2022 citation was a reference defect [P24, L7, L8, L9, L10, L11, L12].
Fourth, the failed DOIs in P25 are corrected to real resolving papers (Sun et al. 2020 at DOI 10.1016/j.buildenv.2020.106842 and Baniassadi et al. 2018 at DOI 10.1016/j.buildenv.2018.05.024), neither of which varies occupancy dynamically in its abstract [L20, L21, L22, L23].
Fifth, BuildOcc exists as arXiv:2609.02729 and Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21192895 as an ATUS-grounded LLM occupant agent platform, occupying the pretraining sequence space in the United States [L51, L52, L53].
Sixth, ecobee Donate Your Data research terms are not retrievable on an open page, requiring institutional application and preventing open redistribution of derived schedules [L48, L49, L50].
The Part A verdicts changed P24 from an unknown competitor to an absent study, corrected both failed references in P25 to real papers, and showed that the BuildOcc record cited in P4 is a real existing preprint [P24, P25, L11, L12, L20, L22, L51].

### B3. Evidence placing A9 at the top

A9 ranks at the top not from inherited consensus but because Part A searches established: (1) no published study of indoor temperatures during winter power outages in Canadian MURBs exists, and the claimed Baba 2022 paper was a citation error [L7, L8, L9, L10, L11, L12]; (2) real DOIs were established for all benchmark priors (Sheng 2023, Sailor 2019, Pulkkinen 2026, Hotchkiss 2026, Sun 2020, Baniassadi 2018), and none models dynamic or demographic occupancy in its abstract [L13, L14, L15, L16, L17, L18, L19, L20, L21, L22, L23]; (3) four dedicated search phrasings found zero survivability studies with dynamic household occupancy [L24, L25, L26, L27]; and (4) A9 directly serves live drafted proposals for two fellowship programmes (NSERC and UC Berkeley) [BRIEF s.5, P36, P37].

### B4. Vulnerability of the top three

* For A9: publication of an EnergyPlus district survivability study coupling dynamic, household-resolved occupant presence to outage indoor temperatures would drop G from 40 to 20 [INFERENCE].
* For A12: publication of an accepted benchmark protocol for membership-inference audits and privacy guarantees on energy or time-use microdata generators would drop G from 40 to 20 [INFERENCE].
* For A2: publication of a study combining national time-use diary presence sequences with building thermal models for district-scale indoor heat exposure would drop G from 40 to 20 [INFERENCE].

## Section E. What this changes in our planning

* Prioritize A9 as the primary subject for 5J: Part A demonstrates that passive survivability with demographically varied occupancy remains unclaimed across both winter and summer outages, directly serving both NSERC and Berkeley application themes [P21, P36, P37, L7-L27].
* Package A12 as a rapid, companion protocol communication: A12 uses assets already held and finalized from 4J's audit without requiring new simulation campaigns, achieving a score equal to A9 [BRIEF s.3, P27, P34].
