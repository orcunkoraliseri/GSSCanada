# RT43. Which occupancy paper that trains a model on our own GPUs is still open, and can be scored without people

## Section A. Direct answer

Two candidate forms are already taken in the literature: occupancy detection from smart meters (B6) is heavily crowded [L8, L15, L25], and language-model household daily planning (B7) is already established in transportation research [L141, L144].
Household load conditioned on survey demographics (B2) is partly taken in energy systems literature [L54, L56].
Pretraining across open time-use corpora (B3) has BuildOcc as an ATUS-grounded neighbor [L2, L4] and violates Rule 4 if framed as cross-national transfer [BRIEF s.11, INFERENCE].
Predicting the next survey cycle (B5) breaks Rule 3 if evaluated against COVID-era stay-at-home shifts [BRIEF s.11, INFERENCE].
Diary-to-meter paired sequence modeling (B1) is open as far as searched [L32, L37], but is constrained by small sample sizes and risks access delays exceeding the four-week window [L173, INFERENCE].
The occupancy-aware EnergyPlus simulation surrogate (B4) is open as far as searched [L99, L109], breaks zero rules of brief section 11, relies entirely on paired simulation runs generated on our own cluster, and can finish training and scoring on a single A100 GPU node in under two weeks [BRIEF s.3, BRIEF s.11, INFERENCE].
We would test B4 first because its training data is generated internally with zero institutional access dependencies, and its scoring is entirely automated against held-out physics simulations [BRIEF s.3, BRIEF s.11, INFERENCE].

## Section E. What this changes in our planning

* **Drop B6 (smart meter occupancy detection) and B7 (language-model daily planning) from consideration (tied to Section B Rows 1 and 2, Section C Rows 2, 3, 5, 6):** These topics are saturated in the smart-grid and transportation literatures [L15, L25, L141]. Proposing another occupancy detector or another LLM agent that writes a daily schedule offers no novelty and invites immediate rejection [L8, L146, INFERENCE].
* **Avoid cross-national pretraining on time use (B3) to prevent repeating 4J (tied to Section B Row 8, Section C Row 1):** Although BuildOcc established an ATUS agent platform in 2026 [L2, L4], attempting cross-national pretraining on ATUS/MTUS to adapt to Canada directly replicates 4J's failing transfer paradigm, which lost to the raked donor null [BRIEF s.3, BRIEF s.11, INFERENCE].
* **Select Form B4 (Occupancy-aware EnergyPlus surrogate) as the primary 5J candidate (tied to Section B Rows 6 and 10, Section C Row 9, Section D Item 3):** B4 solves the critical computational bottleneck of urban building energy modeling by training a Transformer surrogate that takes arbitrary occupancy sequences and outputs hourly end-use loads under frozen building frames [L99, BRIEF s.3, INFERENCE]. It isolates the occupancy effect, requires zero external data approvals, and can be fully trained and validated on the Speed cluster in under two weeks [BRIEF s.3, BRIEF s.11, INFERENCE].
* **Incorporate conformal prediction bounds into the surrogate (Form B9 integration) (tied to Section B Row 6, Section D Item 3):** To address reviewer skepticism regarding surrogate approximation error, 5J can equip the B4 surrogate with conformal prediction intervals, providing guaranteed coverage on hourly thermal loads without human scoring [INFERENCE].
