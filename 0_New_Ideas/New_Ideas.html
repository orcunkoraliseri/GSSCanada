Our papers so far

What each one trained
CENTUS

Italian census and time-use survey. LSTM and Transformer trained for activity and presence. Published, Energy and Buildings 2026.

eSim

Canadian GSS to EnergyPlus, proof of concept; at-home share 63.5 % (2005) to 72.3 % (2022). Published 2026.

1J

Paired EnergyPlus campaign, occupancy varied with the building fixed. No model trained. In revision, J. Building Performance Simulation.

2J

Conditional Transformer (about 29 M parameters) on 64,061 GSS diaries; load shape 2005 to 2030. Resubmission to Applied Energy prepared.

3J

Three-head Transformer for a mixed-use tower (home, office, retail) plus a hotel channel. Final read, Building and Environment.

4J

Open language model (OLMo 3 7B) fine-tuned on European diaries; lost to a pool of real diaries. Final read, Energy and Buildings.

Common thread: we train an occupancy generator and run paired EnergyPlus simulations. The EnergyPlus stand-in uses both, which is why it fits the GPU window best.

Occupancy during harsh events

Who stays home during blizzards, cold spells, ice storms, heat waves, outages, evacuations and stay-at-home orders, and what heating, cooling and backup power they need. EnergyPlus ensembles on Speed with machine learning.
