## 2. Root cause — two independent bugs, both introduced by the Step-9 rollout

### Bug A — multi-zone equipment/lighting injection collapse (FIXED, see §3)

For multi-zone apartment archetypes (**MidRise**, **HighRise**, **OtherDwelling** — buildings
with ~21–49 dwelling-unit zones), the Step-9 "carrier" (one calibrated SHEU household's
equipment/lighting load) was injected into **only the occupancy zone**, while the legacy
`ElectricEquipment`/`Lights` objects were neutralized across **all** zones. Net effect: whole-
building equipment/lighting electricity collapsed to roughly **1/N_units** of its physical total
(measured ~37–99× depending on archetype). Plausibility check: ~7.7 GJ/yr modelled for an entire
21–49-unit MidRise building is one household's consumption, not a whole building's — confirms a
bug, not a deliberate simplification.

**Legacy method** used `Watts_per_Zone_Floor_Area` (W/m², naturally scales with zone size,
physically = "N units, each behaving like this one sampled household"). **Step-9 carrier** used
absolute `EquipmentLevel` (Watts) in one zone only — collapsing N units' worth of load to 1.

### Bug B — untracked / hidden electric end-use, archetype-specific (NOT YET FIXED, see §4)

Independent of Bug A: a compounding **untracked-meter gap** in `idf_optimizer.py`
(`REQUIRED_METERS` / `HOURLY_DETAIL_METERS`, ~lines 188–227) means some electric end-use was
never requested as an `Output:Meter` — invisible to post-processing even though it's baked into
the summed `Electricity:Facility` total (confirmed via `eplustbl.csv` End Uses table, whose rows
sum exactly to the Facility total).

- **MidRise**: the hidden driver is **`WaterSystems:Electricity`** (domestic hot water — 100%
  electric for MidRise, ~80% of building electricity). Normally masked by the (now-collapsed)
  equipment load under Bug A; dominates and "reveals" once equipment shrinks.
- **HighRise**: DHW is **gas** for this archetype (confirmed: `Water Systems` end use = 341.35 GJ
  gas / 0 electric in the test run), so the hidden driver must be something else — confirmed
  2026-07-13 to be an **electric heating component** (94.70 GJ/yr classified under the `Heating`
  end use, alongside 211.43 GJ gas heating). Investigation into the exact object/schedule is
  **in progress** (see §4).

### Scope across cities/archetypes

Confirmed (per user request to check breadth before deciding remediation) that Bug A affects all
4 archetypes across all 6 cities uniformly in *mechanism*, but severity differs:
- **MidRise / HighRise**: both magnitude AND diurnal-shape distortion (most severe).
- **OtherDwelling**: magnitude only, diurnal shape stays clean.
- **SingleD**: single-zone archetype, not affected by Bug A at all (mild/likely-intentional
  recalibration only).
