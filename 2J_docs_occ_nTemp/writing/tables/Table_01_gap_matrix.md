**Reading of the matrix:** The closest single competitor is Chen et al. (2022) — stock-scale, calibrated, activity-resolved — but retrospective (no forecast, no COVID break). Yin et al. (2025) is closest on forecasting non-stationary behaviour but stops at statistical probability modelling with no bottom-up simulation and no stock-scale load shapes. The open cell this study fills is the combination of *forecast to 2030 through the COVID/WFH structural break* **and** *stock-scale paired BEM simulation of the resulting load shape*.

> Note: The matrix lists **external** competitors only. The authors' own prior C-VAE work (Iseri & Hachem-Vermette, JBPS under review; eSim 2026) would satisfy most columns; its delta over the present paper is captured by the four advances described in §1.5 (generator, loads, horizon/validation, attribution), not by these six binary axes.
