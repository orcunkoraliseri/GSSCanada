- **Simulated EUI** values are from the Phase-5-corrected `campaign_N50` campaign (post-Bug-A-fix, full 18-cell re-simulation completed 2026-07-14/15). Exact values, building-level, n=300/archetype (50 HH × 6 cities): 2022 — SingleD 199.99, OtherDwelling 114.86, MidRise 169.57, HighRise 127.78; 2030 — SingleD 204.54, OtherDwelling 116.31, MidRise 164.38, HighRise 125.93 kWh/m² (rounded to integers in the table above). Reproduced via a clean standalone script (`table5_eui_reproduce.py`), not inline computation.
- **v3 (Bug-A-fixed) vs v2 (pre-fix, 2026-06-10) Δ, 2022 basis:** SingleD −3.9%, OtherDwelling −10.1%, MidRise +11.7%, HighRise +9.2%. MidRise/HighRise moving up is the expected direction of the Bug-A fix (both are multi-zone archetypes whose equipment/lighting were previously under-injected into a single zone instead of replicated per-zone). SingleD (single-zone, structurally untouched by Bug A) drifting −3.9% and OtherDwelling drifting −10.1% (**opposite** the Bug-A-fix direction) are **not explained by the injection fix itself** — most likely residual campaign-vintage drift from other intervening pipeline changes (e.g. the 2026-07-09 Step-5 region-tier relink) rather than a sign error, but this has not been independently root-caused. Flagged here rather than silently absorbed into the "corrected" framing.
- **SHEU bands** (unchanged from the prior table — static NRCan reference data, not affected by re-simulation): NRCan *2019 Survey of Household Energy Use* — national central = **Table 3.3b** (Energy Intensity Per Heated Area excluding Garage, by Dwelling Type); regional range = **Table 3.3a** (same metric, by region). Total all-fuels (secondary/site energy), per **heated** area **excluding basement and garage**. Conversion 1 GJ = 277.78 kWh. U-flagged (suppressed) regional cells excluded from the range.
- **Floor-area basis.** EUI denominator is EnergyPlus *Net Conditioned Building Area* (conditioned zones only), matching SHEU's heated-area-excluding-basement/garage basis — confirmed not a denominator artifact (see `deepResearch/EUI_floor_area_reconciliation.md`). For the apartment archetypes, the building-level denominator includes common corridors; re-normalizing to per-dwelling-unit area (×1.11) gives a secondary cross-check: **MidRise ≈188 (2022) / ≈182 (2030)**, **HighRise ≈142 (2022) / ≈140 (2030)** kWh/m² — both archetypes remain inside their SHEU bands under this correction too in both years (HighRise 2022's ≈142 sits closest to its 147.2 upper bound, ~3.6% margin).
- **SingleDetached and OtherDwelling now both fall outside their SHEU regional bands** (SingleD above, OtherDwelling below) — SingleD's above-band reading was already present pre-fix (was +12% in the stale table, now +7.5%/+9.9% post-fix, i.e. *smaller* than before) and is understood as a genuine space-heating-driven elevation (NECB/IECC envelope + Zone-6 weather, not SHEU-calibrated — see prior reconciliation note). OtherDwelling's below-band reading **worsened** from "marginal, ≈6% below" (pre-fix) to "≈15% below" (post-fix) and is the one genuinely new plausibility concern surfaced by this re-derivation; regional averages smooth within-province variance, so this should be reported honestly rather than asserted as a pass.
- **What actually passed validation.** The hard calibration gate is on **per-household end-use kWh/yr** (net-after-fridge anchors; last run 48/48 dwelling-by-year cells within ±2.7% of SHEU, job 954636, 2026-06-08/09, documented as phase-invariant since it tests per-household calibration-target correctness, a concern Bug A's building-wide zone-replication defect did not touch). **This gate has not been re-run against the Phase-5-corrected campaign** — the Step-9 arm-validator scripts (`step9_loadshape_aggregate.py`, `step9_validate_full.py`) are structurally incompatible with the corrected single-arm `campaign_N50` layout (see `Step8_docs/08_09_injection_bug_status.md`, Task #20 entry) and remain an open blocker, unresolved by this table's re-derivation.
