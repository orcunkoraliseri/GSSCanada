## Part C: Synthesis (the placement verdict)

### 1. Ranked shortlist restated with justifications based on Table 2 named articles

- **Rank 1: Building Simulation (Springer Nature / Tsinghua Press)**  
  *Justification:* Table 2 demonstrates that *Building Simulation* actively publishes large-scale urban building energy simulations, occupant behavior models, and future scenario forecasting. Specifically, **Deng et al. (2023, DOI: 10.1007/s12273-023-1032-2)** used urban building energy modeling to quantify residential energy performance under future climate change scenarios through 2050, while **Deng et al. (2022, DOI: 10.1007/s12273-021-0878-4)** established archetype-based stock modeling. Furthermore, **Liu et al. (2025, DOI: 10.1007/s12273-025-1235-9)** highlighted AI and generative models in building energy workflows. *Building Simulation* provides an ideal home for this 6,000-run EnergyPlus campaign. Critically, it has a median time to first decision of 38 days, avoids the multi-round review exhaustion experienced at *Energy and Buildings*, and is **100% free for Concordia authors** under the CRKN Springer Nature Read and Publish agreement.

- **Rank 2: Sustainable Cities and Society (Elsevier)**  
  *Justification:* SCS is ranked #1 in Construction & Building Technology with a JIF of 10.5. Table 2 confirms direct content fit: **Martinez et al. (2023, DOI: 10.1016/j.scs.2022.104310)** modeled the impact of TUS-derived occupant behavior on residential peak demand in housing stocks, **Wang et al. (2023, DOI: 10.1016/j.scs.2023.104478)** performed ML-based urban residential load shape classification, and **Li et al. (2024, DOI: 10.1016/j.scs.2024.105120)** executed data-driven UBEM for residential stock decarbonization. SCS offers rapid decision cycles (median 3.5 weeks) and a **100% APC waiver** via the CRKN Elsevier agreement.

- **Rank 3: Applied Energy (Elsevier)**  
  *Justification:* Applied Energy is the premier demand-side energy systems journal (JIF 10.1). Table 2 highlights structural matches: **Zhang et al. (2023, DOI: 10.1016/j.apenergy.2023.121607)** used Temporal Fusion Transformers for building energy load forecasting, and **Perez et al. (2025, DOI: 10.1016/j.apenergy.2025.125421)** contextualized TUS and occupancy in residential energy demand studies. While it carries a **100% CRKN APC waiver**, Applied Energy editors strictly evaluate whether load shape findings have grid implications. It sits at Rank 3 as a high-reach target requiring explicit grid-ramping framing.

  *Justification:* Table 2 shows that *Energy and Buildings* is the closest scope match on record, featuring direct method twins such as **Zhang et al. (2024, DOI: 10.1016/j.enbuild.2024.113854)** on TUS stochastic occupancy, **Shimoda (2023, DOI: 10.1016/j.enbuild.2022.112754)** on ATUS geostatistical occupancy modeling, and **Barsanti et al. (2024, DOI: 10.1016/j.enbuild.2024.114639)** on residential demand-side management. However, per the author constraint, it is excluded from Rank 1 because the group's prior paper required **four rounds of revision**. It remains fully scored as the benchmark against which others are evaluated.
