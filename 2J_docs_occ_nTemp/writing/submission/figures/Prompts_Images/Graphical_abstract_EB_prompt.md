## 2. The one message

Working from home changes WHEN homes use electricity much more than HOW MUCH they use.

## 3. Layout: three panels, left to right, equal height

White background, thin grey rules between panels, one sans-serif font, all text horizontal,
colour-blind-safe colours (for example navy, teal, orange).

### Panel A (left, about 35 % of width): "Method"

Three boxes joined by arrows, left to right:
1. **Time-use diaries, 2005 to 2022**
2. **Household occupancy model**
3. **Building energy simulation: 4 dwelling types, 6 Canadian cities**

Footer line: **2022 compared with 2030 work-from-home scenarios**

### Panel B (centre, about 35 % of width): "2022 to 2030, main scenario"

Three simple horizontal bars or number tiles, one row each:
- **Annual electricity: +0.12 %** (short, grey)
- **Midday share: +0.73 percentage points** (teal)
- **Load factor: +0.49 percentage points** (teal)

Optional small line above: **Weekday at-home rate in 2022: 4.73 points above its 2005-2015 trend**

### Panel C (right, about 30 % of width): "Take-away"

Bold text, two lines:
**Timing, not sizing**
**Average schedules erase the household spread in peak timing; the household model keeps it.**
