# Deep-Research Results dr_2J-15: 2030 as a Planning Horizon, and the Next Canadian Time-Use Survey (Citation Search)

## 1. Summary Verdict per Claim

* **Claim A (2030 is a recognized planning horizon in energy and buildings):** FOUND.
  Multiple official policy and planning frameworks establish 2030 as the primary milestone target year, including the federal *2030 Emissions Reduction Plan* (ECCC 2022), the Ontario Independent Electricity System Operator's decarbonization pathway (*Pathways to Decarbonization*, IESO 2022), and international roadmaps (*Net Zero by 2050*, IEA 2021).
* **Claim B (Timing of the next comparable Canadian time-use survey cycle):** PARTIAL.
  Statistics Canada's official survey documentation establishes the historical cycle schedule (1986, 1992, 1998, 2005, 2010, 2015-2016, and 2022 under Record Number 4503) and its statutory frequency ("Every 5 years"). However, Statistics Canada has not published or announced a specific next collection year following the 2022 cycle.

## 3. Recommended Citations

### For Claim A (2030 as a Recognized Planning Horizon)
* **Recommended Primary Citation:** Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC, 2022), *2030 Emissions Reduction Plan: Clean Air, Strong Economy*.
* **Recommended Provincial/Grid Citation:** Independent Electricity System Operator (IESO, 2022), *Pathways to Decarbonization*.
* **Recommended Global Citation:** International Energy Agency (IEA, 2021), *Net Zero by 2050: A Roadmap for the Global Energy Sector* (DOI: 10.1787/c8328405-en).

### For Claim B (Timing of Next Comparable GSS Cycle)
* **Recommended Citation:** Statistics Canada (2024), *Time Use Survey*, Statistical Data Documentation System (SDDS) Record Number 4503.
* **Drafting Guidance for the Manuscript Sentence:**
  Because Statistics Canada lists the formal survey frequency as "Every 5 years" but has not announced a fixed calendar year for the cycle following 2022, the manuscript sentence should be phrased carefully:
  > "This study uses 2030 as its scenario horizon because it aligns with federal and provincial decarbonization benchmarks (e.g., Environment and Climate Change Canada, 2022; IESO, 2022) and sits beyond the 2022 General Social Survey on Time Use, for which Statistics Canada operates on an approximate five- to seven-year cycle with no post-2022 cycle date yet announced (Statistics Canada, 2024)."

## 4. Positive Control Result

* **Objective:** Locate the official Statistics Canada survey record page for the 2022 Time Use cycle.
* **Positive Control Status:** SUCCEEDED.
* **Survey Record Identifier:** Statistical Data Documentation System (SDDS) Record Number 4503.
  *(Note: While some internal project documentation refers to "GSS Cycle 37", Statistics Canada officially registers the survey program under SDDS Record Number 4503, titled "Time Use Survey". SDDS 4501 is the GSS Family survey, and SDDS 4504 is the GSS Victimization survey).*
* **Survey Page URL:** https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p2SV.pl?Function=getSurvey&SDDS=4503
* **Instance ID for 2022 Reference Period:** Id=1400713 (https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p2SV.pl?Function=getSurvey&Id=1400713).
* **Reference Period Dates:** Data collection ran from July 16, 2022 to July 15, 2023. Data release date: June 5, 2024. Page modification date: June 4, 2024.
