The manager has already diagnosed the cause; you do **not** need to rediscover it.

`T55/T55_scripts/t55_score.sh` sets only `PY` and never sets either variable, so the checker fell back to a
hard-coded `/usr/local` path that does not exist on the compute nodes. The manager has verified the real
file exists and is the right version: `/speed-scratch/o_iseri/ep_wrappers/Energy+.idd`, 4,448,311 bytes,
first line `!IDD_Version 24.2.0`.

**So this is an environment omission in the checker's wrapper, not a defect in the data, and not a defect in
V3's logic.** Fix it additively and re-run V3. Nothing else.

## 3. The three outcomes — this vocabulary is load-bearing

Every gate and every control in your report must end in **exactly one** of these three words, and the three
must stay distinguishable everywhere they appear:

- **did not run** — the section raised before reaching its own test. Produces **no verdict at all.**
- **ran and did not fire** — the section executed and found nothing wrong.
