### B3 is REJECTED — not because the runs are bad, because the test is built wrong

`t28_b3_wp4_test.csv` has 72 metric-cell rows and **14 carry `inside_t_ci=False`** (and the same 14 carry
`inside_boot_ci=False`). The naive reading — "the 50-home answer sits outside the 200-home interval in 19 %
of cases, so 50 homes is not enough" — is **wrong, and it would have been a serious error to put in the
paper.**

**`B1` itself is what defeats it.** `prefix_ok=True` means **the 50 homes are the first 50 of the same 200**.
The two means are nested, not independent. Writing `mean50 - mean200` in terms of the 200 draws gives

```
Var(mean50 - mean200) = sigma^2 * (1/50 - 1/200) = sigma^2 * 3/200
```

so the difference's own standard deviation is **sqrt(3) times the standard error of `mean200`**. The
`inside_t_ci` flag measures that difference against the `mean200` **confidence interval** — a yardstick
**sqrt(3) too short**. Under perfectly well-behaved sampling the flag should read `False` about
`2*(1-Phi(1.96/sqrt(3)))` = **26 %** of the time. **The observed rate is 19 %, which is BELOW that.**
So the evidence, read correctly, leans *toward* 50 homes being adequate — but **`B3` is not a test and no
verdict may be quoted from it in either direction.** `B3=REPORT` was the right label; the CSV's two boolean
columns are the trap.

**This is the same family as plan §5 item 29 (entry (cd)): a comparison whose two sides are not on the same
basis.** The response is identical — **the band is not widened, because the band was never the problem.**

**What the 14 flagged rows do show, held loosely until T60:** energy totals are largely unflagged while the
load-shape metrics (`mean_peak_hour`, `midday_share`, `evening_ramp_kW_mean`) and especially the
`_delta_2022to2030` changes carry most of the flags. **If that pattern survives the corrected test, it is a
real and reportable limitation** — the paper's energy conclusions and its load-shape conclusions would not
be equally supported at 50 households. **It is not reportable yet.** Several of the flagged rows sit inside
even the n=50 interval on inspection, which is another sign the flag is not doing what its name says.
