### Finding 1 — P2's 0.5 pp band is breached on both arms, by nearly the same amount

Read from `/speed-scratch/o_iseri/2J_revision/T29/out/t29_check.json`:

| λ | `target_wd_pp` (from T26) | `injected_stock_weighted_wd_pp` (from the runs) | `diff_pp` | `flag_over_0.5pp` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 73.2080594433387 | 74.89275507775919 | **+1.6846956344204926** | true |
| 0.0 | 70.84227310158259 | 72.57500276388889 | **+1.7327296623062978** | true |

**The reason this is a diagnosis and not yet a defect:** the two arms are off by +1.68 and +1.73 pp —
almost the same number, in the same direction, under two different λ values. A scenario-construction
error would be expected to scale with λ. A near-constant offset that survives the λ change looks much
more like the two sides of the comparison being computed over **different populations**, and that is a
question about the comparison, not about the schedules.

Here is the specific suspicion, stated plainly so you can test it rather than agree with it.
`p2_target_reached()` in `T29/T29_scripts/t29_check.py` lines 214-258 computes its "injected" number
**only over the sampled households**:

```python
wd = bem[(bem["Day_Type"] == "Weekday") & (bem["SIM_HH_ID"].isin(all_ids))]
per_house = wd.groupby("SIM_HH_ID")["Occupancy_Schedule"].mean()
per_archetype_pp = (per_house_df.groupby("DTYPE")["mean_occ"].mean() * 100.0).to_dict()
stock_weighted_pp = sum(STOCK_WEIGHTS[a] * per_archetype_pp[a] for a in ARCH_NAMES if a in per_archetype_pp)
```

`all_ids` is the set of `sim_hh_id` values the energy campaign actually sampled — 1,198 of them for
λ=0.5 and 1,196 for λ=0.0. The target side is a single population-level number:
`float(tgt[tgt["stratum"] == 1]["target"].mean() * 100.0)`. **If T26's target was computed over the
whole household file, then the gate compares a 1,198-home sample against a 144,465-home population**,
and a 1.7 pp gap would say something about who the sampler picked, not about whether the schedules hit
their target.

**That is a hypothesis. Measure it; do not assume it.**
