**Subject:** Second journal paper: Building Simulation decision and resubmission plan

Dear Caroline,

I hope you are well. I would like to update you on our second journal paper, "From 'How Much' to
'When': Forecasting the Residential Energy Load Shape from a Calibrated Behavioural Occupancy
Time-Series (Canada, 2005–2030)".

Unfortunately, Building Simulation rejected the paper (decision of 15 September 2026, three
reviewers). The reviewers found the research question valuable, but they asked for stronger evidence
and a clearer, shorter paper. Since then, we have improved the paper as follows:

- **Corrected 2030 projection:** we fixed the error in the 2030 occupancy and re-ran the building
  simulations (about 6,000 runs).
- **Scenarios instead of one forecast:** 2030 is now shown under clear work-from-home scenarios
  (the shift stays, partly reverses, or fully reverses).
- **Confidence intervals:** every reported change now comes with a confidence interval, so we only
  claim changes the data can support.
- **Check against measured data:** the simulated hourly load shape is compared with measured 2022
  electricity data for Toronto and Ontario.
- **Comparison with a simpler method:** we show what the household-level model adds over a simple
  average-profile schedule (it keeps the spread of household peak times).
- **Held-out test of the occupancy model:** the model is trained up to 2015 and tested on the unseen
  2022 survey.
- **Clearer paper:** new figures (including end use by hour), equations for each method step, results
  separated from methods, and fewer internal terms.

The main message is now clearer: under the main scenario, annual electricity stays almost flat
(+0.12 %), while the midday share of energy and the load factor rise. The change is about when homes
use electricity, not how much.

We plan to submit the revised paper to **Applied Energy** as a new submission (backups: Sustainable
Cities and Society, then Journal of Building Engineering). If you approve, I will finish the last
edits and submit it. I would be happy to send you the manuscript first if you would like to read it.

Thank you very much.
