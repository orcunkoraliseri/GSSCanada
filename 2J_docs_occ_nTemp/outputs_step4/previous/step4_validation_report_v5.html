Why calibration adjusts only hom30 — not the other two J3 heads. The J3 model emits three outputs per slot: act30 (14-way activity), hom30 (binary AT_HOME occupancy), and cop30 (9-channel co-presence). Phase-8B raking is a marginal-correction step. It was applied to hom30 alone because that head had a real per-(stratum×slot) marginal error (15.37 pp) in the exact space the Step-5/6 validators score, and it sets the People-present fraction that every downstream gain scales by. The other two heads already met their model gates, so raking them would add inter-head coherence risk for little net benefit (see the “raking all three” discussion below the table).

Verdict. For annual / seasonal energy the headroom is negligible: metabolic moves ~2%, and equipment/lighting levels are SHEU-calibrated (identical totals regardless of act30). For load timing / peak demand the equipment shape is genuinely act30-sensitive (~15% mean, ~32% at the morning peak) — a study reporting coincident peaks would benefit, a study reporting energy use would not. Either way the cost is real: three independent marginal rakes would worsen inter-head coherence (a home act30 under a raked hom30 = 0); the principled fix is a single joint per-(cell×slot) rake over (activity × AT_HOME × co-presence) (IPF), preserving temporal transitions — a substantial step (04L proved only the AT_HOME + COP pair). Since the occupancy that dominates energy (hom30) is already exact, this is recorded as future work, not a blocker for the current release.

Section 9 — Addendum: 2005 Cycle Under-Representation in the Census Linkage

Follow-up investigation into the Section 7 “Work by Stratum” chart, where the calibrated 2005 weekday bar (~22% of slots in paid work) sits far below the 2010 / 2015 / 2022 bars (~39%). The root cause is a demographic key-coding gap in the Step-5 census linkage — not a model, activity, or calibration defect.

9.1 — It is a matching (demand) effect, not a supply shortage

In the diary pool (augmented_diaries.csv), 2005 is the largest cycle, yet it wins the fewest matches against the 286,540 Census-2021 agents. Each Census agent draws one diary via np.random.choice among all diaries matching its demographic cell, through a Tier 1→4 fallback hierarchy (05_census_linkage.py).

2005 has ample supply but an expected Tier-1 selection of 0.0% — it never wins an exact match. Its ~9% final share comes only from coarse fallback tiers (55.1% of Census agents fall past Tier-1).

9.2 — Root cause: 2005 PR (province) left in legacy 5-region coding

Of the seven match keys (AGEGRP, SEX, MARSTH, HHSIZE, LFTAG, PR, CMA), six overlap the Census for 2005. Only PR is disjoint:

SourcePR values presentOverlap with Census
Census 202110, 24, 35, 46, 48, 59 (StatCan SGC province codes)—
Pool 2010 / 2015 / 202210, 11, 12, 13, 24, 35, 46, 47, 48, 59 (SGC)MATCH
Pool 20051, 2, 3, 4, 5 (legacy GSS 5-region grouping)DISJOINT
Mechanism. Step-2 harmonization mapped PR to SGC codes for 2010 / 2015 / 2022 but left the 2005 cycle in the old GSS 5-region scheme (1 = Atlantic, 2 = Québec, 3 = Ontario, 4 = Prairies, 5 = BC). Because PR appears in both the Tier-1 key set (all 7 keys) and the Tier-2 “core” set (AGEGRP, SEX, LFTAG, PR, DDAY), every 2005 diary fails both — its PR value (1–5) can never equal a Census SGC code (10–59). 2005 diaries become eligible only at Tier-3 (AGEGRP, SEX, DDAY) and Tier-4 (day-type only), where province is dropped.

9.3 — Impact and status

  • BEM aggregate unaffected. hom30 is raked to the exact per-(stratum×slot) AT_HOME marginal after linkage, so occupancy schedules are correct regardless of the cycle mix. no gate impact
  • Two real defects. 2005 is under-weighted ~3× (30% of supply → 9% of matches), and its diaries are placed without province (age/sex only) — any province-specific 2005 signal is scrambled. Combined with un-raked act30, this produces the anomalous 2005 paid-work bar in Section 7.
  • Not fully recoverable. The GSS 2005 PUMF releases only a 5-region variable (not exact province, for confidentiality): Atlantic = NL/PE/NS/NB, Prairies = MB/SK/AB. An exact SGC PR for 2005 cannot be reconstructed — hence it was left un-mapped.
  • Options. (a) crosswalk the 5 regions to province groups and relax the PR match to that grouping; (b) drop PR from the match keys for cross-cycle consistency; or (c) document the caveat (recommended pre-submission — BEM results already hold via the AT_HOME rake).

Known Limitations (documented, accepted)

  • Activity not calibrated. Raking touches hom30 only; act30 is untouched, so the Work proxy still over-fires (6.2 = 3.27 pp, expected-FAIL). Out of scope for occupancy-marginal calibration.
  • Per-cell-slot co-presence not raked. Raw COP max = 19.85 pp per-cell-slot; the Spouse marginal gate already passed (2.23 pp), so the conditional Spouse rake was skipped. (04L proved joint AT_HOME + COP raking feasible if a future gate requires it.)
  • Coherence cost. Down-flips create hom30 = 0 against a home act30 in ~1.82% (2022) / ~2.07% (2030) of slot-records. BEM-harmless — BEM keys occupancy off hom30, not act30.
  • Composition artefact. The 4.48 pp raw 2.2/6.1 residual is pure DDAY_STRATA day-type composition (GSS 92.6% WD vs population 71.4%), composition-held to 0.0037 pp — a reporting caveat, not a model error.
  • 2005 cycle under-represented in linkage. The 2005 PR key was left in legacy GSS 5-region coding (disjoint from Census SGC codes), so 2005 diaries fail Tier-1/2 matching and are ~3× under-weighted. BEM-harmless (occupancy raked post-link); full analysis in Section 9.
