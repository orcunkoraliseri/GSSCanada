Question: has anyone already trained a learned stand-in for EnergyPlus that takes an occupancy
sequence as input and is scored on the occupancy effect (the difference between two households in
the same building)?

## What this settles (updated 2026-09-28, after the outside search RT45 was checked)

* No work read so far trains a surrogate on an occupancy sequence and scores it on the occupancy effect.
* Scoring a surrogate on differences is NOT new in general (row 4). Our claim is its occupancy version, with a blind control.
* Hourly residential stock surrogates are NOT new (rows 5, 6; CityTFT 2025, `10.1016/j.apenergy.2025.125712`, title only).
* Row 3 is abstract-level: low risk, input list unread. O-2 closed on it by the author (2026-09-28); full-text re-check at Step 8.
* The outside search logged no queries, so "nobody has used an occupancy sequence as surrogate input" is NOT established; see `Prompts/deepResearch/VETTING_RT45.md` section 6.

## What this settled (first pass, before RT45)

* The two coupling papers that weakened B4 in the RT43 vetting do NOT train a surrogate. Both run
  EnergyPlus directly with a stochastic occupancy input. B4 is not taken by them.
* He et al. 2015 is the closest precedent for the paired design (same house, several occupancy
  profiles, different demand) and must be cited as such. It never scores that difference.
* Still open: Li et al. 2021 (a real EnergyPlus surrogate; inputs unread) and anything published
  since 2019 that these three do not cover. Outside search prompt T45
  (`5J_docs_occ/Prompts/deepResearch/`) asks for both.
