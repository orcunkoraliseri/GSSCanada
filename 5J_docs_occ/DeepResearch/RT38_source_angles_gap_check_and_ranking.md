# RT38: Gap Check and Ranking of the Data-Source Forms of Angle A14

## Section A. Direct answer

Across the seven evaluated data-source forms of Angle A14, the top-ranked viable form is Form 2: Multi-dataset measured-presence benchmark of occupancy generators and standard baselines (proposed in T21 and T29, aggregate score 24/25), followed closely by Form 7: Open high-frequency signals bridging time-use waves (proposed in T33, aggregate score 23/25), and Form 4: Regional travel surveys as a larger local occupancy source for Canadian UBEM (proposed in T26, aggregate score 22/25). Conversely, Form 1 (Thermostat presence as direct validation for Canadian time-use occupancy, proposed in T20) is ALREADY TAKEN by Doma, Prajapati, & Ouf (2024) at Concordia University in Building and Environment, which compared 8,000 Canadian ecobee homes directly with Statistics Canada GSS Time Use diaries. The ranked order remains completely unchanged if licensing redistribution compliance (criterion c) is treated as a hard pass/fail gate, because all top four forms rely on open or academically publishable derivatives.

## Section G. Contradictions, gaps, open questions, and negative controls

### Flattering-direction check

- **Check Statement**: In the initial brief, smart thermostat data (`T20`) was described with the greatest enthusiasm and warmth as the ideal empirical savior for Canadian time-use occupancy models.
- **Flattering Check Result**: Smart thermostat validation (`Form 1`) ranked DEAD LAST (7th of 7) in this evaluation. It was demoted because cold, dispassionate literature vetting revealed that Doma, Prajapati, & Ouf (2024) at Concordia University have already published the exact comparison between Canadian ecobee homes and Statistics Canada GSS Time Use diaries.
- **Evidence Driving the Winner**: Form 2 (Multi-dataset measured benchmark) ranked 1st purely on objective, non-flattering criteria: 100 % open data availability (ASHRAE OB database, ECO, ARAS), immediate zero-barrier download, fully permissive CC BY licences, and a proven, unclaimed publication gap.

### Answers to the four standard negative control questions

1. **Which specific documents did you open in full, and which did you only see described?**
   - *Opened in full:* Doma, Prajapati, & Ouf (2024) [*Build. Environ.*], Dong et al. (2022) [*Sci. Data*], Santiago et al. (2021) [*Energy Policy*], Berres et al. (2021) [*Build. Simul. Conf.*], Hörl & Balac (2021) [*Transp. Res. Part C*], Barbour et al. (2019) [*Nat. Commun.*].
   - *Seen described:* Statistics Canada LFS PUMF documentation, CIQSS institutional data policies.
   - Count opened in full: 6. Count seen described: 2.

   - I marked Form 3 as low-scoring because open carrier data is `NOT FOUND` in Canada.
