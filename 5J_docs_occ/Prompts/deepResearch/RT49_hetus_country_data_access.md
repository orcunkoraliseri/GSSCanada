# RT49. European Time-Use Diary Datasets: Country Access and Terms Assessment

## Section A. Direct answer

Across the 18 European countries that participated in HETUS 2010 and the 11 countries that have delivered data to HETUS 2020, only two national statistical offices provide open microdata downloads containing episode-level diaries: Spain (INE) and Italy (ISTAT). Four additional sources are obtainable now through immediate academic registration: the Multinational Time Use Study (MTUS / IPUMS Time Use, providing harmonised episode files across eight European countries), the UK Time Use Survey 2014-2015 (UK Data Service SN 8128 under Safeguarded End User Licence), and Germany's Public Use Files for ZVE 2012-2013 and 2022 (Destatis FDZ PUF). Eight countries and the centralised Eurostat HETUS 2010 Scientific Use File collection are obtainable through a scientific research application, but Eurostat access requires an estimated 12-week lead time because Concordia University is not yet recognized as a research entity. Nine access routes are not obtainable for this study, either because microdata access is restricted to on-site safe centres or remote virtual enclaves that cannot execute the project's external EnergyPlus simulation pipeline (Netherlands CBS, Austria AMDC, Finland FIONA, Hungary KSH), because national regulations exclude non-EU applicants without European hosts (German Destatis SUF), or because microdata have not yet been released (HETUS 2020 round not available before 2027; France 2025-2026 fieldwork ongoing). The fastest route to expanding the study to more European countries is the Multinational Time Use Study (MTUS / IPUMS Time Use), which provides immediate online extract access to 10-minute and 15-minute episode files for France 2009, Spain 2009, Italy 2008, the UK 2014, Finland 2009, Austria 2008, Hungary 2009, and the Netherlands 2005 with linked household composition. The negative control dataset "HETUS 2010 public-use microdata file, release HETUS-PUF-2010-v9, Eurostat" was queried and confirmed NOT FOUND.

Rankings are assigned strictly according to data access rules, eligibility of a non-EU postdoctoral researcher at Concordia University, turnaround time, cost, and compatibility with the study's computational requirements (running an external EnergyPlus building energy simulation pipeline on the researcher's own cluster).

### Group A. Obtainable now (open download or immediate registration, all four needs met)

1. Spain (INE, Encuesta de Empleo del Tiempo 2009-2010): Open download directly from INE under CC BY 4.0. Meets all four needs: 10-minute diary slots, main and secondary activities, location at home, all household members aged 10 and over diarised with household roster, day type (weekday/weekend) and season, regional identifier (Autonomous Community), and survey weights.
2. Italy (ISTAT, Indagine Uso del tempo 2013-2014): Public-use file (micro.stat / file per la ricerca ad uso pubblico) downloadable from Istat portal. Direct download of microdata files. Meets all four needs: 10-minute slots, main and secondary activities, location, all household members aged 8 and over diarised, day type, NUTS1 region, and weights. ## Section G. Contradictions, gaps, open questions, and your own negative controls

### Disagreements, ambiguities, and literature gaps

* Positive controls verified: Spain (INE EET 2009-2010) and Italy (ISTAT Uso del tempo 2013-2014) were verified against active landing pages and download links. Spain is an open ASCII download under CC BY 4.0; Italy is an open public-use micro.stat file under Istat legal terms. Both confirm positive control specifications.
* Negative control verified: The dataset "HETUS 2010 public-use microdata file, release HETUS-PUF-2010-v9, Eurostat" was queried across Eurostat's microdata portal, publications, and Eurobase metadata. It returned NOT FOUND. Eurostat provides only Scientific Use Files (SUF) for accredited research entities and pre-calculated aggregate tables on Eurobase; it does not issue public-use files for HETUS.
* Resolution of French Progedo request n. 38663: The researcher requested "lil-1065, Emploi du temps (version pour Eurostat) 2009-2010". Investigation of the Progedo ADISP catalogue API (data.progedo.fr) revealed that lil-1065 has availability status avlstatus = SUS (Suspended). The active, orderable record for the French 2009-2010 time-use survey is lil-0695 ("Emploi du temps - 2009-2010", Version 6), which carries avlstatus = FPR (Fichier Production Recherche) and includes the full diary carnet. The request was approved without lil-1065 because lil-1065 is suspended in the archive, not because the researcher was ineligible.
* Lead time gap for centralised Eurostat access: While Eurostat offers HETUS 2010 microdata covering 17 countries in a single collection, Concordia University is not currently on the list of recognised research entities. Obtaining access requires first applying for entity recognition (~4 weeks) and then submitting a research proposal (~8 weeks), creating a ~12-week minimum delay. This exceeds the project's four-week GPU window.
* Remote enclave incompatibility with building simulation: Several national statistical institutes (Netherlands CBS, Austria AMDC, Finland FIONA, Hungary KSH) restrict microdata exclusively to secure remote or on-site desktop environments. These enclaves prohibit the installation of external simulation engines (such as EnergyPlus) and forbid exporting unit-record schedules to the researcher's Canadian cluster. Consequently, these countries cannot be used for the study's simulation pipeline regardless of applicant eligibility.
