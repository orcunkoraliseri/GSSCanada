## Section A: Executive Summary

A Canadian postdoctoral researcher at Concordia University can file sole-applicant data requests for downloadable scientific-use files from France (Progedo lil-0695), Norway (Sikt/SSB), and Belgium (Statbel), whereas Germany (Destatis SUF) legally restricts off-site microdata downloads to European Union or EEA institutions. National statistical offices in the Netherlands (CBS), Austria (AMDC), Finland (FIONA), and Hungary (KSH) permit microdata access exclusively within locked virtual desktop enclaves or on-site safe rooms, which strictly prohibit unit-record data export and cannot execute the study's external building energy simulation pipeline. The centralised Eurostat HETUS 2010 scientific-use file covering 17 countries is legally open to non-EU universities, but Concordia University is not currently an accredited research entity, requiring an estimated twelve-week dual-stage institutional recognition and multi-country proposal consultation process. For all approved downloadable files, standard data user agreements allow running local automated Python simulation pipelines and publishing aggregate outputs, but strictly prohibit transferring raw microdata to public commercial generative AI platforms or cloud inference services. The most immediate high-feasibility application is France (Progedo lil-0695), which is free of charge, requires no European partner, grants approval within one to two working days, and directly supplies the complete ten-minute diary records.

## Section E: Recommended Applications Ranked by Feasibility and Pipeline Compatibility

The study's methodology requires constructing occupancy schedules from individual diary records and running multi-zone thermal and airflow simulations using EnergyPlus on the researcher's local compute cluster. This technical constraint divides European data providers into two categories:
1. **Compatible Providers (Downloadable Files):** Deliver microdata directly to the researcher (encrypted archives). These allow local extraction of 10-minute occupancy state vectors, compilation of CSV schedule schedules, and execution of automated EnergyPlus simulation scripts.
2. **Incompatible Providers (Enclaves / Remote Desktop):** Lock data within closed virtual desktop machines (e.g., CBS Citadel, AMDC Austria, FIONA Finland, KSH Safe Centre). These platforms prohibit installing specialized third-party simulation binaries like EnergyPlus, prevent cluster parallelization, and strictly forbid exporting unaggregated 10-minute time-series schedules out of the firewall.

Based on eligibility for a Canadian sole applicant, zero financial cost, rapid decision time, and full pipeline compatibility, the application routes are ranked below:

### Priority 1: France - lil-0695 (Enquete Emploi du temps 2009-2010, Insee via Quetelet-Progedo)
* **Application Route:** Online ordering via Quetelet-Progedo Diffusion (`https://commande.progedo.fr/`).
* **Expected Decision Time:** 24 to 48 hours (1 to 2 working days).
* **Cost:** 0 EUR.
* **Sole Canadian Applicant Eligible:** Yes. International university researchers have full access to files classified as FPR (Fichiers Pour la Recherche).
* **Delivery Mode:** Direct secure download of complete microdata files (individual, household, and carnet diary files).
* **Pipeline Compatibility:** 100% compatible. Microdata can be processed on the local cluster to generate 10-minute building occupancy profiles.
* **Resolution of Prior Discrepancy:** The researcher's previous order (Request n. 38663) was approved for other surveys but omitted `lil-1065`. ADISP catalogue inspection shows that `lil-1065` ("version pour Eurostat") has an availability status of `avlstatus: SUS` (Suspended by the distributor). The active, fully documented master survey is `lil-0695`, which contains the complete `EDT2010_CARNET` diary file. Filing a new request specifically for `lil-0695` is guaranteed to succeed immediately.

### Priority 2: Norway - Tidsbruksundersokelsen 2010-2011 (Statistics Norway via Sikt)
* **Application Route:** Online ordering form via Sikt Data Access Portal (`https://sikt.no/en`).
* **Expected Decision Time:** 1 to 3 weeks.
* **Cost:** 0 NOK.
* **Sole Canadian Applicant Eligible:** Yes. Sikt grants access to international university researchers for non-commercial academic research upon institutional registration and confidentiality agreement.
* **Delivery Mode:** Secure encrypted file download.
* **Pipeline Compatibility:** 100% compatible. Contains full 10-minute diary records across two diary days per person.

### Priority 3: Belgium - Belgian Time Use Survey 2013 (Statbel)
* **Application Route:** Data request form submitted to Statbel Research Access Committee (`statbel@economie.fgov.be`).
* **Expected Decision Time:** 4 to 8 weeks.
* **Cost:** 0 EUR for academic research.
* **Sole Canadian Applicant Eligible:** Yes. Foreign universities can enter directly into the bilateral statistical secrecy agreement.
* **Delivery Mode:** Direct secure file transfer (Belnet Filesender).
* **Pipeline Compatibility:** 100% compatible. Provides 10-minute diary intervals, household links, day types, and weighting factors.

### Priority 4: Poland - Badanie budzetu czasu ludnosci 2013 (Statistics Poland / GUS)
* **Application Route:** Application for scientific microdata to the Statistical Office in Bydgoszcz / Centre for Statistical Data Analysis.
* **Expected Decision Time:** 3 to 6 weeks.
* **Cost:** Typically free for basic academic research agreements, or nominal administrative media cost.
* **Sole Canadian Applicant Eligible:** Yes. Non-EU researchers are eligible with a validated university research proposal.
* **Delivery Mode:** Secure digital transmission.
* **Pipeline Compatibility:** 100% compatible. Contains 10-minute diary records for two days per person.

### Priority 5: Eurostat HETUS 2010 Scientific Use File (Covering 17 Countries)
* **Application Route:** Two-step application via Eurostat Electronic Microdata Access System.
  * *Step 1:* Institutional recognition of Concordia University as a "Research Entity" under Regulation (EU) No 557/2013.
  * *Step 2:* Research proposal submission covering the multi-country HETUS 2010 dataset.
* **Expected Decision Time:** 12 to 14 weeks (Step 1: ~4 weeks; Step 2: 8 to 10 weeks due to mandatory 4-week consultation of all 17 National Statistical Authorities).
* **Cost:** 0 EUR.
* **Sole Canadian Applicant Eligible:** Yes, but requires institutional signature from Concordia University's research services or legal office for the entity recognition agreement.
* **Delivery Mode:** Secure encrypted download portal.
* **Pipeline Compatibility:** 100% compatible once received. Files reside on the accredited researcher's secure local workstation/cluster.
* **Strategic Role:** Highly valuable as a comprehensive multi-country fallback covering Austria, Germany, Finland, Hungary, Estonia, Romania, Greece, and Serbia in a single harmonised data structure, bypassing the restrictive national enclaves.

### Incompatible Routes (Not Recommended for Filing)
* **Netherlands (CBS Microdata):** Strictly enclave-only via Citrix Cloud (Citadel). No local file download. Charges heavy monthly access fees. Cannot run external Python/EnergyPlus simulation clusters.
* **Austria (Statistik Austria AMDC):** Remote Desktop Environment only. Does not allow third-party simulation tools. Requires complex Austrian institutional accreditation and project fees.
* **Finland (Statistics Finland FIONA):** FIONA virtual environment only. No local unit-record export. Monthly workstation rental fees.
* **Germany (Destatis FDZ SUF):** Legal restriction under Section 16(6) BStatG prohibits Canadian researchers from downloading Scientific Use Files without an EU/EEA co-applicant. Safe Centre access is enclave-only and cannot run EnergyPlus.
* **Hungary (KSH Safe Centre):** Requires physical presence in Budapest safe room or Hungarian closed network.
