# RT47. Reference support: check the reference list, and find sources for the methods and claims that have none

## Section A. Direct answer

All 17 references currently in the draft reference list were audited against official registry metadata in Crossref, OSTI, INE, ISTAT, and ASHRAE, but none is completely correct as written because all 14 journal and technical report entries lack author initials, several omit full paper titles, three lack DOIs and need institutional catalogue records, and entry 2 gave a truncated title. Entries 1 (ASHRAE Guideline 14-2002), 6 (INE 2011), and 8 (ISTAT 2016) require official catalogue records and landing URLs in place of missing DOIs, while entries 3 through 5, 7, and 9 through 17 require additions of full author initials, verbatim registry titles, and precise volume, issue, and article number designations. For Part 2, all 8 named methods and frameworks found opened original foundational sources, including the formal TCN architecture (Bai et al., 2018), Transformer encoder (Vaswani et al., 2017), scikit-learn histogram gradient boosting and its LightGBM algorithmic basis (Pedregosa et al., 2011; Ke et al., 2017), ERA5 reanalysis (Hersbach et al., 2020), cluster bootstrap (Cameron et al., 2008; Davison and Hinkley, 1997), HETUS guidelines (Eurostat, 2009), official EnergyPlus 23.1 documentation (U.S. DOE, 2023), and PyTorch (Paszke et al., 2019). For Part 3, all 9 context statements were confirmed with opened empirical or review literature from 2005 to 2025 supporting each claim with page-specific quotations. Both positive controls (Crawley et al., 2001 and Loga et al., 2016) matched their Crossref records across every metadata field, while the negative control DOI (10.1016/j.enbuild.2099.000001) returned HTTP 404 DOI NOT FOUND as required.

### Note on ASHRAE Guideline 14-2014

A 2014 edition of ASHRAE Guideline 14 exists: **ASHRAE Guideline 14-2014: Measurement of Energy, Demand, and Water Savings**, published December 18, 2014 (ISSN 1049-894X, American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers, Atlanta, GA). In building energy modeling practice and secondary literature, the calibration acceptance criteria for hourly data remain CV(RMSE) <= 30 % and NMBE within +-10 % (and for monthly data CV(RMSE) <= 15 % and NMBE within +-5 %). Because the full official standard document is proprietary and paywalled by ASHRAE, clause 4.2.8 / 6.3.3.4 could not be opened directly on a public server: `CLAUSE NOT OPENED` (catalogue record verified).
