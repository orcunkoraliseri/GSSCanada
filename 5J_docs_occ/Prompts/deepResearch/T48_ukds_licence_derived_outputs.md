# T48. UK Data Service licence: do outputs of a model trained on UK time-use diaries count as UK-derived data?

Paste `00_MASTER_BRIEF_5J.md` first, then `_RESPONSE_TEMPLATE.md`, then this prompt, in one fresh session. **Run it alone**
(it does not depend on T46 or T47). Written 2026-10-01 by the 5J manager, for the author's licence decision.
Your files are `RT48_ukds_licence_derived_outputs.md` and `RT48_pages.log` (one line per page, PDF or record you opened: URL,
what it was, full / partial / title only, date). Save both in this folder.

**This is a question about licence terms and published guidance, not a request for legal advice.** Quote the documents. When
you interpret, label it `INTERPRETATION` and say what wording it rests on. `NOT FOUND` and `NOT STATED IN THE LICENCE` are good
answers.

## Why we are asking

The paper uses Spanish and Italian households only. Their diary days, however, were not taken straight from the Spanish and
Italian surveys: they were produced by the diary generator of the author's previous study. That generator is a fine-tuned
language model trained under leave-one-country-out: the Spanish days come from the model trained on the **UK and Italian**
diaries, and the Italian days from the model trained on the **Spanish and UK** diaries. The UK diaries are the UK Time Use
Survey 2014-2015 from the UK Data Service (study number SN 8128, Sullivan and Gershuny, 2023), downloaded by the author under
the End User Licence (EUL), version 16.00 dated 25 February 2026 as we read it.

From those generated days the study built hourly schedules, ran 9,269 EnergyPlus simulations, trained a neural surrogate, and
now reports aggregate results (pass counts, R2 values, district totals). No UK diary row, and no UK respondent identifier,
appears in any of these outputs. The author must decide whether the paper can be published and its outputs shared as they are,
or whether the UK Data Service must be asked first. We need the facts to make that decision.

Our own reading (to be checked, not repeated back to us): clause 2 limits use to the purposes registered in the user's
Project Information; clause 4 forbids giving the data or any derived dataset, synthetic ones included, to anyone who is not a
registered user; clause 5 forbids online data tools, generative AI included, "in connection with" the data without written
permission; clause 11 requires citation and acknowledgement; clause 12 requires sending the publication details; clause 18
requires destroying copies and derived data at the end of the access period. Treat every one of these as unverified.
