Mine:
1. Epoch = 400,000 // 512 = 781 steps (399,872 windows). Cosine decays per step over epochs x 781 (no warm-up); early stop or time cap ends sooner. Time cap checked after each epoch.
2. Training uses a straight-through clip (loss sees max(output, 0 kWh) but the gradient is not zeroed below 0); validation and prediction use a plain clip.
3. Loss = mean over windows and 24 h of the SUM over the 3 standardised targets; pair term likewise on (ŷA-ŷB) vs (yA-yB); loss = level + lambda x pair. Pair term computed and printed for lambda 0 too (not used in the gradient).
4. One day per batch (all 256 pairs share it); pair and day drawn uniformly with replacement from a CUDA generator seeded 1.
5. Neighbour channels: mean of people / appl_w over the flats of the set, standardised with the people / appl_w statistics, multiplied by the flag (absent set -> 0); flag channels 0/1 not standardised. Static s_* standardised with the development mean/SD (constant columns SD->1; saved in norm_static.json and best.pt); c_* one-hot as is.
6. TCN block = LayerNorm (channels) -> causal dilated conv k3 -> GELU -> 1x1 conv -> residual. Transformer = pre-norm encoder layers, GELU. Final LayerNorm before the head in both.
