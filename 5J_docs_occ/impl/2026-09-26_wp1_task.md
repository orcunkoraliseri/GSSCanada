## Goal
Draw the human-labelling sample: Montreal 2,000 permit texts (dev 400, calibration 800, test 800) and
Toronto 600 (calibration 300, test 300), stratified, with inclusion probabilities, plus 400 texts marked
for double labelling, plus the labelling sheets. **The author labels the texts; a colleague labels the 400.**

## Fixed rules (do not change)
1. **Universe.** Residential permits only, using the WP0-A residential rule for Montreal (accent-insensitive; 420,572 permits) and the WP0-A Toronto rule (729,339 residential) **plus the 2,394 extra "Residential"/"House" rows** counted as residential. Text must be non-empty after trimming. Toronto: keep the cleared-file row for duplicate permit numbers (as WP0-A did); strip the "HVAC -" prefix into a separate column, keep the original too.
2. **Keyword group** (used only to build strata and quotas; the labeller never sees it). Accent-insensitive, lower-cased, priority order, first match wins:
   HP: `thermopompe|pompe a chaleur|heat pump|mini-split|minisplit|ductless` ·
   AC: `climatis|air conditionn|air conditioning|a/c unit|central air` ·
   F: `chauffage|fournaise|convecteur|plinthe|mazout|chaudiere|boiler|furnace|baseboard|heating system` ·
   I: `isolation|isoler|isolant|reisol|insulat` ·
   W: `fenetre|vitrage|porte-fenetre|window|glazing|patio door` ·
   N: `nouveau batiment|nouvelle construction|construction d.un|new house|new dwelling|new building|neuf|new construction` ·
   D: `demolition|demolir|demolish` ·
   NONE: none of the above.
   (Regexes are a first pass to make rare events appear; they are not the reader. Print how many texts in the universe fall in each group, per city.)
3. **Strata** = keyword group × decade of `date_emission` (Toronto: the issue-date column; buckets: before 2000, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, 2020 and later) × building type. Montreal building type = `description_categorie_batiment` collapsed to at most 4 classes (write the mapping down); Toronto = its dwelling/structure-type column collapsed to at most 3 classes, or dropped if none is usable (decide, write it down). Merge any stratum with fewer than 5 universe permits into the nearest-decade stratum of the same group and type, and record every merge.
4. **Quotas per split, by keyword group** (share of the split): HP 12 %, AC 8 %, F 10 %, I 14 %, W 18 %, N 8 %, D 6 %, NONE 24 %. If a group has too few permits, take all of them and give the rest to NONE; record it. Inside a group, spread the quota over its strata proportionally to the universe count (largest-remainder rounding), never taking more than the stratum holds.
5. **Splits are disjoint.** Draw in the order test, calibration, dev; a permit used once is removed from the universe. Montreal: test 800, cal 800, dev 400. Toronto: test 300, cal 300, no dev.
6. **Inclusion probability.** For each drawn row: pi = (rows drawn in its stratum for its split) / (permits in the stratum still in the universe when that split was drawn); weight = 1/pi. Save the stratum population counts so that weights can be checked: within a stratum and split, the weights sum to the universe count at draw time.
7. **No duplicate texts.** Compare a normalised text (lower case, accents removed, text between « » or quotation marks removed, digits kept, spaces collapsed). If a draw repeats a normalised text already drawn anywhere in the sample of either city, discard it and draw the next permit from the same stratum. Record how many redraws happened; note in Decisions that this slightly favours rare texts.
8. **Double labelling.** Mark 400 texts (300 Montreal, 100 Toronto) as `double_label = 1`, drawn at random within the sample, stratified by keyword group in proportion to the sample, spread over the splits in proportion to their sizes.
9. **Seed.** One integer seed, fixed in the script and written in the impl doc; results must reproduce exactly on a second run (check the sha256 of the manifest across two runs).
