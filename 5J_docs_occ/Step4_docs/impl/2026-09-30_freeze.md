## Decisions (not in the task doc; manager to confirm) - all also written in gates_frozen.md
1. G5J.2 pass rule: the median run of the cell inside CV(RMSE) <= 30 % and |NMBE| <= 10 % (spec only said "median and share reported").
2. "Above the floor" = |sum dEP| > max(k*sqrt(2)*floor, 0.001 kWh): a rounding guard because the files have 8 significant digits.
3. TH floor = largest of the other classes (no TH replicate input exists).
4. "B1" for the skill in the perturbation table = EnergyPlus + noise at 30 % of the hourly SD ("weak B1"); with B1 at 10 % (as the good stand-in) the good stand-in cannot beat B1, so val 2.0 could never pass. The null uses the manager's construction (independent copy of the good one).
5. Building unit of the bootstrap = building id (the same id in several climates is one unit; conservative). Household unit = country + household id. Seed = 20260930 + 1000*country index + 100*class index.
6. Noise in the stand-ins: independent per target and hour (total electricity has its own noise), not clipped at 0. "Run hourly SD" = SD over all dwelling-hours of the run.
7. Building-mean stand-in = mean over the good predictions of all runs of the same building and climate in the split, per flat index; training-mean stand-in = mean over the development split per climate, class, hour of year. The control C of the other rows = the building-mean stand-in; row 5 uses the good stand-in as its own control.
8. R2 = 1 - SSE/SST with SST around the pooled mean of dEP over all pairs of the cell (not the squared correlation).
9. Pair ordering (A = earlier run of the pair in the group list) is arbitrary; R2 and sign agreement do not depend on it.
