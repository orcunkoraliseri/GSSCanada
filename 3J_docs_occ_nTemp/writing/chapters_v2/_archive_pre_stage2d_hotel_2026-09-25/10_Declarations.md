# Data availability

This study uses the Statistics Canada General Social Survey time-use public-use microdata files (2005, 2010, 2015 and 2022 cycles) and the 2021 Census of Population public-use microdata file. These files are available from Statistics Canada under its licence terms, which do not allow the authors to redistribute them. The provincial hotel-occupancy series are published by the Institut de la statistique du Québec and by CBRE and Travel Alberta. The derived occupancy schedules, building model files and scripts that support the findings of this study are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.

# Ethical approval

This study involves no experiments on human or animal subjects. It uses anonymized public-use microdata released by Statistics Canada and published provincial tourism statistics.
