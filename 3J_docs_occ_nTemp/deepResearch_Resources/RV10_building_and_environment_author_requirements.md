# RV10. Everything Building and Environment requires of a submission, from the journal's own pages

## Section A. Direct answer

This report audits every author requirement, manuscript limit, artwork specification, peer review policy, declaration, scope boundary, and publishing cost for submissions to Building and Environment (Elsevier). All 32 items have been checked against official Elsevier and ScienceDirect policy pages, the journal's Guide for Authors, and the CRKN institutional agreement database. The audit establishes that Building and Environment uses single-anonymized review, does not impose a numeric word limit on abstracts or full manuscripts for original Research Papers, imposes no cap on figure count, and is 100 percent APC-waived for Concordia University corresponding authors under the CRKN agreement. However, five figures currently at 140 dpi are non-compliant with the publisher minimum of 300 dpi for halftones, 500 dpi for combination art, and 1000 dpi for line drawings, and must be regenerated before revision.

### Blocking list

These are the items where the manuscript as described is currently out of compliance, ranked from most severe to minor formatting:

1. **Figure resolution (High Severity):** Five of the eleven main figures are currently at 140 dpi. Elsevier requires 300 dpi for halftones, 500 dpi for combination art, and 1000 dpi for bitmapped line drawings. While a single compiled PDF is acceptable at initial submission under Your Paper Your Way, raster figures below 300 dpi risk desk-triage flagging or blocking at revision. All plots and schematics should be regenerated as vector PDF/EPS or 600+ dpi TIFF/PNG.
2. **Highlights character cap (Moderate Severity):** Highlights must consist of 3 to 5 bullets, each strictly capped at a maximum of 85 characters including spaces. If the draft highlights exceed 85 characters per bullet, the submission system will block upload.
3. **Mandatory Declarations block (Moderate Severity):** The submission package must contain:
   - CRediT authorship statement using the 14 standard role terms.
   - Competing Interest declaration (explicitly stating "Declarations of interest: none" if null).
   - Data Availability statement.
   - Generative AI declaration statement placed before the References if AI tools assisted in drafting.
4. **Keywords count (Low Severity):** Maximum of 6 keywords, avoiding plural forms and composite phrases.

### One-line answers to the three session-action questions

* **(a) Abstract word limit:** **NO.** The Guide for Authors specifies "A concise and factual abstract is required" but states **no numeric word limit** for original Research Papers; the 272-word abstract is fully compliant and does not need to be cut.
* **(b) Figure count limit:** **NO.** The Guide for Authors states **no numeric limit** on the number of figures or tables in a Research Paper; 11 main figures plus a graphical abstract is completely within policy.
* **(c) Blinded manuscript requirement:** **NO.** Building and Environment operates a **single-anonymized review process**, so author names, affiliations, and unmasked self-citations must remain in the primary manuscript file.

- **Review Model Confirmation:** Third-party academic forums occasionally describe Building and Environment as double-blind; however, the official Elsevier Guide for Authors explicitly states: "This journal operates a single anonymized review process." No blinding or masked title pages are necessary.
- **CRKN Hybrid Eligibility:** Building and Environment is confirmed on the CRKN 2024-2026 eligible hybrid journal list (ISSN 0360-1323), granting full 100 percent APC waivers to Concordia University corresponding authors.
