### F — Channel consistency cross-product (ported + extended)

Conservation checks directional (not hard equality); pairwise overlap ≈ 0 informational; the four channels' products mutually independent (changing one bundle axis leaves the other channels' products byte-identical — MD5 insulation check, the Leg-2 pattern).

**F.1 REWRITTEN 2026-07-30** (see Progress Log — this was the most serious finding in the 2026-07-30 gate sweep): the 2030 branch used to be a hardcoded dict of *prose* describing a human's manual observations from the 2026-07-23 build session, fed straight into an unconditional `pass` and published as `CONFIRMED (build-session evidence)` — it computed nothing at runtime; a past manual observation laundered into a present automated PASS, and this is the gate that would have to catch a channel-separability violation (the property the whole 3×3×3-via-9-builds campaign design depends on).
