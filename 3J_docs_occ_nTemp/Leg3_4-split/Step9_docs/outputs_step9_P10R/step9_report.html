Read the basis before the number. EUI is given on two bases and they are not interchangeable. CFA divides a channel's energy by its own conditioned floor area — the thermodynamic reading, and the one the as-modelled PASS bands apply to. GFA-share adds the channel's area-prorated share of service/MEP and exterior lighting and divides by its share of gross — the basis SCIEU/CEUD stock figures are quoted on, used here for the INFO comparison only. CFA typically reads higher. Every floor area behind these numbers is parsed per cell from the IDF and the EnergyPlus SQL, never taken from a documented constant.

Caveats carried to the manuscript

  1. Asymmetric pathways to energy (D-20). The three commercial channels modulate People, Lights and Equipment — three routes from occupancy to energy. Residential drives People only (OD-7D: the Step-7 residential product has no equipment/lighting columns). Cross-channel comparisons of occupancy-to-energy sensitivity are therefore structurally biased against residential, which is the thesis subject. Intra-channel comparisons — bands, cities, eras — are unaffected: the asymmetry is common mode.
  2. Damped scenario response is by design, not a weak result: only People/Lights/Equipment gains are modulated, while densities and the code baseline are untouched.
  3. Dual basis. CFA vs GFA-share differ systematically; never compare one to the other.
  4. Retail staff and hotel guests are outside the GSS frame — retail staff are logged as AT_WORK, hotel guests are not sampled at all. Their loads live in the NECB baseline being modulated.
  5. Hotel amenity zones are unmodulated in v1 (OD-6).
  6. Ground-level EPW on a supertall — no altitudinal temperature or wind gradient.
  7. Standalone-prototype EUI bands applied to stacked-tower channels. The as-modelled PASS bands were derived from freestanding prototypes. A channel occupying floors inside one tower has almost no roof, ground or (for interior zones) facade load, and shares a centrally sized plant, so it is expected to read lower. See gate S9-BASIS. No threshold was relaxed; the gate still fails against the band as locked, and whether that band applies here is an open decision.
  8. Residential evening ENERGY peak is not reproducible in Leg 3 (gate S9-RESID-EVENING). The occupancy still rises in the evening — the injection flips the channel from midday-dominant (NECB code, ratio 0.22) to evening-dominant (GSS, ratio ~2.8, gate S9-INJECTION) — but under OD-7D that behaviour has no route into energy, because residential lights and plug loads stay on the flat NECB baseline. Any Leg-3 claim about residential evening energy demand would be unsupported by this model.
  9. Cross-era comparability: each cycle's channel products come from that cycle's GSS pool, so the longitudinal comparison is population-level, not a paired design.
  10. Step-6 era-axis calibration bias (Defaut 4) is still open. The IS_SYNTHETIC fraction rises 0 % → 44.6 % → 100 % along the very era sequence §R4 compares. Read §R4 as provisional until that is closed; the band and sensitivity axes are unaffected (common mode).
