## 0. One paragraph

Four Step-4 gates — `G4.1` (within-stratum variance ratio), `G4.3` (shuffled-prefix cross-entropy), `G4.6` (adapter merge drift) and `G4.12` (within-stratum shuffle) — return FAIL on every fold of Leg 4 (`es` 1274884, `uk` 1274964, `it` 1281612) and on Leg-5 `es` (1286209). ### 3.2 `G4.3` — the sign is right, the magnitude is short

In all four runs `CE permuted > CE true`. The model **is** using the prefix; permuting it costs `0.068`-`0.106` nats/token. The gate asks for `0.15`.

🔴 `G4_3_MIN_CE_RISE_NATS_PER_TOKEN = 0.15` is the **only** band in `4thJ_step4_thresholds.py` in this group that carries **no recorded justification**. `G4_4_MIN_MI_RATIO` has a paragraph explaining why one half. `G4_12_MIN_CE_RISE` has a paragraph explaining why it reuses `G4.3`'s number. `G4_1_STATISTIC` carries an explicit ASSUMPTION block. `0.15` itself has none. That is recorded as a **provenance gap**, not as an argument that the number is wrong.

Also unexplained and small: `1`-`2` prefixes per run are **unchanged by the shuffle** (a permutation can fix a point). At `--ce-n 256` that is under 1 % and cannot account for a `0.05` nats/token gap, but it is a dilution of the contrast and is noted rather than dismissed.

### 3.3 `G4.6` — a real drift, 3-7x a band

The drift is genuine (floor `0.000e+00`). The open question is entirely whether `1e-4` is the right band for a **bf16** LoRA merge over 16-20k positions, where `max` over that many positions is an extreme-value statistic and rises with the number of positions compared by construction. 🔴 Note Leg-5 `es` compares the **same 20103 positions** as Leg-4 `es` and reads `7.279e-04` against `3.471e-04` — so position count alone does not explain the leg difference. Not investigated further here.

### 3.4 🔴 `G4.12` — the shuffle swaps diaries that share FIVE OF SIX prefix fields

This is the sharpest thing in the document and it is read out of the code, not inferred from numbers.

```
TH.PREFIX_FIELDS = [country, strat_age_band, strat_sex, strat_hh_type,
                    strat_econ_status, strat_day_type]        # thresholds.py:88

def stratum_key(d):                                            # diagnostics.py:97
    return (d["country"], d["strat_age_band"], d["strat_sex"],
            d["strat_hh_type"], d["strat_day_type"])
```

`stratum_key` is `PREFIX_FIELDS` **minus `strat_econ_status`**. `G4.12` then re-pairs each prefix with another diary's body **from the same stratum cell** and measures the CE rise. Two diaries in one cell therefore agree on **five of the six** prefix fields; the swap can change **at most one prefix token**.

The gate asks that this move CE by `0.15` nats/token — the same amount that permuting the **entire** prefix (`G4.3`) is asked to move it, and permuting the entire prefix only achieves `0.068`-`0.106`. Measured CE rise for the one-token version: `0.0053`, `0.0037`, `0.0043`, `-0.0000`.

🟢 **MEASURED 2026-08-23 (evening), job `1286553` — see §8. The mechanism is confirmed and it is worse than this section predicted.** `P1` and `P2` hold exactly: across 200 within-cell permutations on all four scored runs, the five key fields differ in **zero** pairs and the maximum prefix Hamming distance is **1 of 6**. And **55.42 %-63.80 % of the pairs the shuffle makes have a prefix Hamming distance of ZERO** — for roughly six diaries in ten, `G4.12` re-pairs a prefix with a different person's day and the model's **input does not change by a single character**. The gate then asks the model to have noticed.

🔴 **What this is and is not.** It is a statement about the **check's design**. It is **not** a finding that the model conditions correctly — `G4.3` fails on the full-prefix permutation too, and that one has nothing to do with `stratum_key`. And per §6 it authorises **no threshold change**.

Note what is **not** in doubt: `G4.12`'s MI arm passes on three folds, and the shuffle genuinely moves 589-595 of 600 diaries with **zero** stuck in singleton cells, so the vacuity guard is clean.

### 3.5 `G4.1` — the band, and a recorded assumption underneath it

Band `[0.80, 1.25]` on the ratio of generated to real within-stratum variance of `at_home_share`, scored on the 6 strata reaching `N >= 100`. 🔴 The statistic itself is an **ASSUMPTION** recorded in `thresholds.py:19-26`: neither the step doc nor the val doc defines "within-stratum variance" concretely, and `at_home_share` was chosen by the implementer. The direction of failure is **not stable** — `end=lower (collapse)` on two Leg-4 folds, `end=upper` on `it`, `end=both` on Leg-5 `es`, and it flips between epochs of the same run. 🔴 A gate that fails in both directions on the same model is not measuring one defect, and that instability is itself the thing to explain.

### 🔴 `P3` was specified wrong, and that is my error, not the data's

`P3` asked a **cell-level** question — are there cells that are entirely one economic status? The answer is a clean **no**: every one of the six cells contains a mix. But the quantity that actually bears on the gate is **pair-level**, and the script measured it too: **how many of the pairs the shuffle makes leave the prefix unchanged.** That is `55.42 %`-`63.80 %`.

Both facts are true and they are not in tension — a cell can be mixed and still hand most of its members a partner with the same economic status. `P3` as written tested a sufficient condition for the effect and missed the necessary one. **The effect is larger than `P3` would have detected**, which is exactly why the mis-specification has to be recorded rather than quietly replaced with the number that worked. Recording it is also the reason it can be trusted: had the pair-level figure come back at 5 % the temptation would have been to keep looking, and it did not.

The corrected statement of §3.4, which is what should be quoted:

> `G4.12`'s within-stratum shuffle can change **at most one of six** prefix fields, and for **55-64 % of diaries it changes nothing at all**. The gate then requires this to raise cross-entropy by `0.15` nats/token — the same amount asked of `G4.3`, which permutes the **entire** prefix and achieves only `0.068`-`0.106`.

### One more thing the run surfaced, not looked for

All four generated sets have **exactly 6 cells of exactly 100**, with **zero** singletons. That is `--gen-stratified-k 6 x 100`, so the shuffle operates inside six very large cells rather than many small ones. It makes the vacuity guard trivially clean (`n_stuck = 0`) — 🔴 so **`G4.12`'s clean `stuck = 0` line is a property of the sampling design, not evidence that the strata are well populated**, and it should not be read as the latter.

### 9.2 🔴 `D-S4-10` — `G4.6`, and the fact that this investigation had it wrong

§3.3 of this document asked whether `1e-4` is defensible for a **bf16** merge over 16-20k positions.
🔴 **That question is wrong in both halves, and it was already recorded as wrong on disk before
I wrote it:**

* the merge is **not** measured in bf16 — `4thJ_step4_train.py` calls `model.float()` **before** the

  the **same kernel with the same reduction order against itself**. Zero was guaranteed by

  nothing about what `G4.6` measures.

🔴 **And the measurement needed to rule the gate already existed.** `4thJ_step4_g46_alpha_sweep.py`
ran as job `1274944` on **2026-08-19** under author ruling `D-S4-3`, and had been sitting at
`4J_step4/g46_alpha_sweep_1274944.json` marked `"REPORTED, NOT ACTED ON"` ever since. Scaling the
adapter's `B` by `alpha`, at a **fixed** 20103 positions:

| `alpha` | `max_logit_diff` | verdict at `1e-4` |
|---:|---:|---|
| `1` | `3.471e-04` | FAIL |
| `0.1` | `1.034e-03` | FAIL |
| `0.01` | **`1.238e-03`** | FAIL |
| `0.001` | `6.428e-04` | FAIL |
| `0` | **`0.000e+00`** | ✅ PASS |
| `1` (repeat) | `3.624e-04` | FAIL |

🔴 **The drift does not shrink as the adapter shrinks.** Cutting the delta 1000-fold leaves it
**at or above** the `alpha = 1` value. This is the **plateau** branch: a floor of order `10^-3`,
independent of how much the adapter learned, sitting an **order of magnitude above the `1e-4` band**.
Both of the sweep's controls are clean — `alpha = 0` returns exactly `0.0`, and the two `alpha = 1`
rows agree to 4.4 %.

🔴 **Consequence: `G4.6` at `1e-4` is unsatisfiable for any adapter that trained.** The only
passing row is an adapter that learned **nothing**. On its own scale the FAIL is the outcome that

recorded as a **pre-registration defect**, with the sweep as the evidence.
