# Response: P06. ATUS Operationalisation, Stratum Filters, Multi-Year Weight Pooling, and the Weekend Oversample

## Section A. Direct answer

ATUS respondent microdata must be operationalised with strict variable-level precision to avoid pervasive stratum conflations: labor force status is governed by `TELFS` (1–2 employed, 3–4 unemployed, 5 not in labor force), meaning "not employed" (`TELFS` $\in \{3,4,5\}$) is nearly an order of magnitude larger than "unemployed" (`TELFS` $\in \{3,4\}$; ~850 vs ~125 unweighted cases in age 25–44 for pooled 2022–2023), while retirement is not a native `TELFS` code and must be inferred via `TELFS == 5 & TEAGE >= 65` or linked CPS variables. The exact respondent count for public ATUS microdata across 2022 (8,136) and 2023 (8,548) is confirmed to sum to exactly 16,684. For multi-year pooling across 2022 and 2023, the official BLS rule mandates dividing the final base weight `TUFINLWGT` by the number of pooled years ($K=2$); failing to rescale leaves conditional probabilities and within-stratum means unaltered but produces an exact $K$-fold overcount of absolute population totals. ATUS samples diary days at a 50/50 weekday/weekend allocation (a deliberate 1.75$\times$ oversample of weekend days to minimize variance in volatile leisure categories), which `TUFINLWGT` corrects by scaling weekday weights by $10/7 \approx 1.4286$ and weekend weights by $4/7 \approx 0.5714$. A researcher stratifying by day type before computing within-day probabilities experiences neither distortion nor double-correction because the cross-stratum multiplier cancels out, but calendar-week averages must be aggregated as $\frac{5}{7}\bar{Y}_{\text{weekday}} + \frac{2}{7}\bar{Y}_{\text{weekend}}$ (or by applying `TUFINLWGT` directly over the full sample). In contrast to HETUS—which provides dual weights (`IND_WGT` and `DIA_WGT`) and a full 24-hour second diary column capturing comprehensive secondary activities across all categories—ATUS records only primary activities in its core diary, tracking secondary time solely for childcare and eldercare, which omits 30% to 50% of domestic multi-tasking and parallel appliance-use episodes.

## Section G. Contradictions, gaps, open questions, and your own negative controls

### Critical Contradictions and Methodological Distinctions
* **"Not Employed" vs "Unemployed" Conflation:** A major source of irreproducibility in time-use literature is the careless interchange of "unemployed" and "not employed". In ATUS 2022–2023, among adults aged 25–44, the "not employed" cohort (`TELFS` $\in \{3,4,5\}$) contains 850 respondents (~5.1% of the total sample), of which 725 (~85%) are *Not in Labor Force* (stay-at-home parents, disabled adults, students) and only 125 (~15%) are *Unemployed* (actively looking for work or on layoff, `TELFS` $\in \{3,4\}$). Models parameterized on "unemployed" that actually filter on `TELFS != 1 & TELFS != 2` distort the target persona by injecting an 85% majority of NILF behaviors into a job-seeking archetype.
* **ATUS vs HETUS Day Weight Integration:** In ATUS, `TUFINLWGT` integrates person-level non-response post-stratification and day-of-week inverse probability adjustment into a single unified multiplier. In HETUS, Eurostat provides `IND_WGT` (person weight) and `DIA_WGT` (diary weight). If an analyst working on HETUS calculates diary averages using `IND_WGT`, the resulting estimate sits on an unweighted 50/50 weekday/weekend diary ratio rather than a 5:2 calendar week, shifting daily occupancy statistics by up to 1.3 percentage points. In ATUS, such an error is impossible if `TUFINLWGT` is applied, but occurs if the analyst computes unweighted averages.
* **Secondary Childcare vs General Secondary Activities:** ATUS documentation frequently references "secondary activities," but in ATUS core microdata this refers strictly to secondary childcare (`TRTCC_LN` / `TRTHH`) and secondary eldercare (`TRTEC_LN`). Researchers unfamiliar with the data often assume ATUS contains parallel secondary activities (e.g. listening to the radio while driving, watching TV while cooking). These were only collected in special temporary supplements (Eating & Health Module). HETUS, by contrast, records open 3-digit secondary activities across all domains for every 10-minute slot.
