## 2. The finding that IS real — arm (b)

Arm (b) held everything constant (same adapter, same prompts, same scorer, same
600 diaries) and changed **only the generation seed**, `TH.SEED` -> `20260824`.

| window | seed 1 | seed 2 | spread | pre-registered expectation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| evening | 0.8193 | 0.9610 | **0.1418** | < 0.10 |
| morning | 0.5441 | 0.5823 | 0.0383 | < 0.10 |

The evening window moves **0.142 by luck of the seed alone**. Morning is fine.

The gate's bar is `ratio >= 0.50` (`G4_4_MIN_MI_RATIO`). So on `es` the evening
number sits 0.32 above the bar and wobbles by 0.14 — it passes either way. But
the wobble is **1.4x the whole tolerance we pre-registered**, and it is not
uniform across the two windows, so we cannot claim it is small in general. On a
fold that lands near the bar, the seed alone could decide PASS or FAIL.

Why: the evening window is where the model is least constrained by the prefix,
so its mutual-information estimate has the most sampling noise. 600 diaries is
not enough to average it out.

## 4. THE QUESTION FOR YOU

**G4.4 passes on all three folds, but its evening number is worth +/- 0.07 of
pure seed noise. How do we report it?**

### (a) Declare the noise, change nothing  — MY RECOMMENDATION
Report every G4.4 evening ratio with the measured seed spread attached, as a
band, not a point: `es` evening `0.82-0.96 (2 seeds)`. State in the paper that
the evening window carries a seed sensitivity of 0.142 at N=600 while the
morning control carries 0.038, and that all folds clear 0.50 by a margin larger
than the noise. No re-run, no code change, no threshold change.
- Cost: zero compute.
- Honest: the wobble is published, not hidden.
- Precedent: this is the same shape as the Step 5 ruling where fidelity ships as
  a band per fold rather than a point.

### (b) Re-generate every fold at K seeds and report the mean
Run `uk` and `it` (and a third `es`) at extra seeds, report the mean of K, and
declare the gate on the mean.
- Cost: 2 more generation jobs per fold, several GPU-hours each.
- Buys: a tighter number.
- Risk: the mean is a **new basis** for a gate that is already scored on all
  three folds. That is a basis change after scoring, which is the thing we
  refused to do for `D-S4-11`. I do not think we can take (b) without
  contradicting that ruling.

### (c) Raise N per generation from 600 to, say, 2000
- Cost: same order as (b), and also a basis change.
- Same objection as (b).

### (d) Mark G4.4 NOT DECISIVE and drop it from the claim
Keep it as a diagnostic, remove it from the scored board.
- Too strong. It passes with margin on every fold; the noise does not reach the
  bar. Throwing away a passing gate over 0.14 is a bigger distortion than
  reporting the 0.14.
