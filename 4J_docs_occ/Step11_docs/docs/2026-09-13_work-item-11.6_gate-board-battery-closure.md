## `G11.6` FAILS at stock scale, honestly, consistent with Step 9's own record

`G9.6` already shipped FAIL at Step 9's n=100 (band never moved,
`4thJ_09_enduseLoads_val.md`). `G11.6` inherits that same +/-15 % band by
design and fails again at stock scale, for the same kind of reason: three
appliances (`tv_2`, `vcr_dvd`, `tv_receiver_box`) land 74-84 % BELOW the

enough of the driving activity to support the published cycle count at the
published cycle length, in both folds. This is the same mechanism `G11.12`'s
own investigation found (laundry recorded as a secondary HETUS activity,
suppressed under the already-ruled `D-S9-1`): two independently-scored gates,
fed by the same trigger loop, land on the same root cause. No re-derivation
of CREST's published counts and no threshold change was made to chase a PASS.
