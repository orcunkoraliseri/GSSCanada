## 2. WHAT "UNINJECTED" MEANS HERE, AND WHY NO NEW MODEL WAS BUILT

`D-S8-2` item 5 was ruled **(c)** and pre-registered on 2026-08-21:

```
phi_int(t) = (1 - f) * 3.0  +  f * 3.0 * g(t) / mean_year(g(t))        f ∈ {0.00, 0.15, 0.30, 0.50, 1.00}
```

🟢 **`f = 0` IS the control** — an endpoint of the pre-registered sweep, not a separately constructed
model. That was the whole point of ruling (c): it removes the *"the control was built differently"*
objection outright. A flat, constant 3.0 W/m² is exactly TABULA's `EU.SUH`/`EU.MUH` `phi_int`
(`FINDING 57`), and it is exactly what the 8.1 IDFs already contain — `OtherEquipment E_PHI_INT` on
`SCH_ALWAYS_ON`.

🔴 **Therefore this campaign wrote no IDF.** Writing one would have re-introduced the very objection
`D-S8-2`(c) was ruled to remove. The 88 archetype IDFs were run **byte-identically** to the 8.1
artefacts, and the selftest re-hashes all 88 on disk afterwards to prove nothing drifted (`C20`).

### 3.1 Why every cell was run twice

`D-S8-1` was ruled **(a)** on 2026-08-20: `G8.1`–`G8.4` are **reproducibility** gates, reference = *a
re-run of the same cell*, thresholds unmoved. That reference did not exist, so the campaign makes it
exist. The re-run is a genuinely independent invocation — **fresh process, different output
directory, different working directory, freshly copied IDF** — because the defect class `D-S8-1`(a)
named is stale-output reuse and path-dependent behaviour, and a re-read of the same directory would
have measured nothing.

🔴 **Stated plainly, because a green number can lie by omission: `G8.1`–`G8.6` all read EXACTLY 0.**
Max |value| over 88 cells is `0` on NMBE monthly, NMBE hourly, CV(RMSE) monthly, CV(RMSE) hourly,
peak magnitude and peak timing. EnergyPlus is deterministic; that is what a clean re-run must give.
**These four gates are tripwires, not accuracy measurements**, the ±5 % / ±10 % / 15 % / 30 %
thresholds sit five to thirty percentage points away from where any honest re-run lands, and they can
only ever fire on gross wiring failure. They earn their keep by being *seen failing* (§6), and the
scorer prints this caveat rather than letting `NMBE = 0.000 %` be read as an accuracy claim.

### 4.3 The one mechanism that is MEASURED rather than hypothesised

`FINDING 119` again, from the other end. TABULA counts gains **only inside its own heating season**,
and that season is recoverable from the workbook: `q_int × 1000 / phi_int` gives the season length in
hours. For `ES.ME` it returns **539 h — 22.4 days**, which is `Tab.AuxCalc.Climate`'s own
`HeatingDays` for that region.

An hourly model with a 20 °C set-point heats **whenever the zone falls below it**, and in Spain it
does so for **2 901 h — 5.4×** TABULA's season. **The two methods are not measuring the same
season**, and Spain is where that difference is decisive. In the UK (1.05×) and Italy (1.15×) the
seasons nearly agree, so season length explains nothing there — those two folds are below TABULA for
some other reason, and this campaign does not claim to have decomposed it. What it does claim is §5.

### `FINDING 122` — the simulation timestep is a free parameter, and it is country-correlated

`es` −4.13 %, `uk` **+5.63 %**, `it` −3.66 %, over all 88 cells (ranges −10.12…−2.30, +2.19…+6.29,
−4.30…−2.35). 🔴 **The sign differs by fold** — the UK moves the other way from Spain and Italy — so
this does not divide out of a cross-fold ratio. Same class as `FINDING 120`'s weather station, and
`Timestep, 6` was never ruled by anything; it is a default that arrived with the IDF writer.

### `FINDING 123` — the ground temperature nobody ruled is worth a third of Spain's heating

No `Site:GroundTemperature` object is written, so **EnergyPlus uses its own default of a constant
18.0 °C** — and says so, once, in every one of the 88 error files. Replacing it with the EPW's own
monthly means moves annual heating by **+33.52 % (`es`), +1.72 % (`uk`), +15.71 % (`it`)** — a **20×
country spread**. 🔴 **In Spain that single invisible default is worth more than switching the entire
internal gain off is worth in the United Kingdom (+19.68 %).** `D-S8-4`'s brief already corrected the
record that closing the weather item does *not* give the model ground temperatures; this measures
what that costs.

### `FINDING 124` — a warning that belongs to one weather file and one fold

**All 32 Italian cells, and no others**, raise `Temperature out of range [-100. to 200.]
(PsyPsatFnTemp)`, input temperature −107.10 °C, once per run, at the same timestamp. Isolated by
measurement rather than by argument:

* a **Spanish** IDF run on the Torino EPW **reproduces** it; an **Italian** IDF on the Valencia EPW
  does **not** → it belongs to the weather file, not to any building;
* every field EnergyPlus reads in that EPW is in range and carries **zero** missing-value sentinels
  (dry bulb −5.6…33.2, dew point −15.4…21.7, RH 17…100, pressure 96 313…99 372, IR 182…421, and no
  row with dew point above dry bulb);
* at **`Timestep, 1` it disappears entirely** → it is an intermediate value inside a sub-hourly
  iteration, not a datum;
* the hourly series across that timestamp is smooth and the run completes with 0 severe errors →
  it does not corrupt the result.

🔴 **`D-S8-4`'s weather gate scored 12 ok / 0 FAILED and could not have caught this**, because it is
not a sentinel, not out of range in any field, and only exists once EnergyPlus interpolates. Recorded
rather than suppressed, which is the whole of `V8.f`: this is 1 occurrence against 88 of the benign
ground-temperature warning, and ranking warnings by frequency would have buried it.

### `FINDING 125` — the ceiling on this paper's own channel is measured, and it is below the figure the step document quotes

`D-S8-2` item 5 holds the **annual mean of `phi_int` at exactly 3.0 W/m² at every level of `f`**. The
sweep therefore *redistributes* internal gain in time and can never add or remove any. `D4` measures
what that entire gain is worth: switching it off moves annual heating by **+40.5 % (`es`), +19.7 %
(`uk`), +20.1 % (`it`)**.

🔴 **So the whole occupancy channel is bounded above by those numbers** — a redistribution of a gain
cannot move annual heating by more than removing the gain does. `4thJ_08_bemSimulation.md` quotes
*"15 to 50 % on annual space heating demand"* as *"the size of the effect this paper is
manipulating"*. **The top of that range is unreachable in two folds of three, and the bound is
country-correlated.** ⚪ This says nothing about **peak** demand, which is a different quantity, is
not bounded by an annual-mean constraint, and is where the same literature puts 100–300 %. Expectation
management for 8.5, set before 8.5 runs rather than after it disappoints.
