## 0. The summary a reader needs before any number

🔴 **The occupancy channel is a PEAK channel, and the annual channel is empty.** Over the full
pre-registered sweep `f = 0 → 1`, the median cell moves **+1.82 % (`es`) / −0.04 % (`uk`) /
+0.06 % (`it`)** on annual heating and **+6.38 / +4.54 / +3.96 %** on peak. `FINDING 125` capped the
whole channel at +40.5 / +19.7 / +20.1 % by switching `phi_int` off entirely; the sweep does not
remove energy, it moves it, and this is where inside that cap it lands.

🔴 **In Spain the annual effect does not exceed the parameter nobody registered.** The between-diary
standard deviation of annual EUI at `f = 1.00` is **1.88 %** against a median effect of **+1.82 %**.
In the UK and Italy the annual effect is an order of magnitude below the diary spread. On **peak**
the effect exceeds the spread in all three folds (6.38 vs 3.74, 4.54 vs 2.58, 3.96 vs 1.96), which
is the only reason a peak result is reportable at all.

🔴 **The campaign was built twice, and the first build was wrong in two ways that left no trace in
the energy.** The diaries were the **Leg-4 pilot**, whose own records stamp themselves
`LEG-4 PILOT -- NOT REPORTABLE`, and the schedules' calendar did not match the model's. Both are
recorded below with the guard that now refuses each, and both guards were **seen firing on the exact
artefact that was about to ship**.

⚪ `prereg.md` untouched, md5 `e4243e07cdd80c9c846b91f40e3e8c45`, verified before and after. No
threshold moved. No checker edited to make a gate pass.

### 1.1 🔴 The free parameter this campaign refused to choose

Nothing anywhere in this project pre-registers **which** generated diary drives **which** archetype.
There are 100 diaries per fold and 88 cells. Picking one diary per cell would have put an undeclared
free parameter between the schedules and every number the paper reports — which is exactly what
`FINDING 120` caught on the weather station, where the "just a file name" turned out to be worth
5–11 % of heating demand.

`FINDING 120`'s precedent is to **measure** the parameter rather than choose it. So every cell is
driven by a declared **ensemble**, what is reported per (cell, `f`) is the ensemble with its spread,
and **the spread is printed beside every effect in every table**. `FINDING 134` below is what that
decision returned.

### 1.2 The scenario path is 8.4's, imported and not re-implemented

`tools/4thJ_step8_scenario.py` — the module work item 8.4 probed. The injector re-points
`OtherEquipment E_PHI_INT` at a `Schedule:File` carrying
`m(t) = (1-f) + f·g(t)/mean(g)`, on a **`PhiMult` (0–100)** type limit rather than `Frac`
(0.0–1.0, which EnergyPlus clips: the multiplier reaches **2.90** on this campaign), with
`Interpolate to Timestep = No`. The cache is the campaign's own skip-if-done index, so `G8.9` is
live in the campaign and not only in 8.4's probe.

## 2. 🔴 Two defects the first build was about to ship

### 2.1 `FINDING 130` — the diaries were the LEG-4 PILOT, and nothing could reach the Leg-5 pools

`tools/4thJ_step7_schedules.py` composed its pool path as
`generated_leg4_<fold>_<arm>.jsonl` with **the leg hard-coded**. Every schedule bundle on disk —
and therefore every diary the injected campaign first wired into EnergyPlus — came from the
600-diary Leg-4 pool whose own records carry `provenance = "LEG-4 PILOT -- NOT REPORTABLE"`. The
Leg-5 pools (`Olmo-3-1025-7B`, 5,200 diaries per fold, **the reported leg**) had been on disk since
2026-08-24 and no code path could reach them.

* **Fixed additively.** A `--leg` argument whose **default is `leg4`**, so every invocation written
  before it existed reproduces byte-for-byte — **verified: 100 of 100 presence files identical** to
  the bundle on disk, before and after the change. Step 7's own schedule selftest: **52 ok / 0
  FAILED**.
* **Guarded.** The campaign now **refuses** a bundle whose manifest provenance contains
  `NOT REPORTABLE`, and the refusal was **seen firing** on `leg4_es_independent_seed1`.
* **What the leg is worth, measured on the ladder rather than assumed:** back-off full-depth share
  rises from **0.578 / 0.623 / 0.635** (Leg-4) to **0.804 / 0.875 / 0.916** (Leg-5) for `es`/`uk`/`it`.

### 2.2 `FINDING 131` — the schedule's calendar was not the model's. `FINDING 99`, realised.

The 8.1 IDFs declare `RunPeriod ... Sunday, !- Day of Week for Start Day` — a choice made in 8.1
**before any schedule existed** and never reconciled with one. The bundles were emitted on the
survey years (`es` 2010, whose 1 January is a **Friday**). So every synthetic Saturday landed on a
Thursday in the simulation, for fifty-two weeks, and the energy result looked entirely normal.

`D-S7-8`(a) had ruled that the schedule year is *"the first calendar year of whatever twelve-month
weather window `D-S8-2` item 6 fixes"*. `D-S8-4` then made that window a **TMYx.2009-2023**
composite, which has no calendar year — so the ruling lost its referent and the IDF's own
`RunPeriod` became the only calendar in the model.

* **Fixed** by re-emitting on **2017**, the nearest non-leap year whose 1 January is a Sunday. Every
  such year gives an identical day-type sequence; the year is a consequence, not a choice.
* **Guarded** by **`V8.i`**, which reads the start day **out of the IDF** and the year **out of the
  bundle's own manifest** and refuses a mismatch. Seen firing on the exact artefact that shipped:
  *"leg4_es_independent_seed1 was emitted on calendar 2010, whose 1 January is a Friday, and the
  IDF's RunPeriod starts on a Sunday."*
* 🔴 **A guard that refuses a defect does not say what the defect was worth**, and "negligible" is
  not something anyone gets to assume. Measured (`tools/4thJ_step8_calendar_probe.py`, 60 runs, one
  archetype per fold, `f = 1.00`, the misaligned arm run with the guard deliberately bypassed for
  that purpose and no other):

  | fold | annual EUI | peak |
  |---|---|---|
  | `es` | **−0.116 %** | **+1.266 %** |
  | `uk` | −0.037 % | +0.371 % |
  | `it` | +0.018 % | −0.388 % |

  Small in magnitude — and **the sign differs by fold on both quantities**, which puts it in the
  LOCO channel, and in Spain **+1.27 % of peak is a fifth of the entire occupancy peak effect this
  campaign reports**. It is recorded as a limitation that was closed, not as one that did not matter.

### 2.3 `FINDING 132` — the pre-registered "exactly 3.0 W/m²" was true of the generator and false of the artefact

`multiplier_series` asserts the annual mean of `m(t)` is 1.0 to 1e-9 — **of the list in memory**.
`write_multiplier_csv` then wrote it at `%.6f`, and the file EnergyPlus actually reads had a mean of
**1 ± 4.01e-07**, i.e. `phi_int` = 3.0000012 W/m² rather than the pre-registered 3.0. Physically
nothing; as a claim, false, and of exactly the class this project hunts — a transform checking its
own numbers instead of its own output.

Found by a check that reads the CSVs on disk rather than the generator's assertion. **Fixed
additively** (a `decimals` argument whose default stays 6, so 8.4's artefacts and the md5s in
`probes_step8.json` reproduce byte-for-byte; 8.5 asks for 10) and the campaign re-run. Residue now
**4.24e-11 against a bound of 5.0e-11 derived from the write format**, never fitted to the number
that came out.

### 3.1 Where each side of `G8.12` comes from

The val doc's own warning: *"if it reads the schedule from the same in-memory object the injector
wrote, it is comparing the injector's numbers against the injector's own reading and cannot fail."*
So the **left** side is re-opened from the `in.idf` EnergyPlus actually read — `OtherEquipment
E_PHI_INT` located by name, its Schedule Name resolved to a `Schedule:File`, that object's File Name
read off disk — and the **right** side is rebuilt from the **published Step 7 diary on disk** at the
declared `f`. Neither side is a number the transform reported about itself. The **assignment** arm
is separate from the value arm, because a transform that re-points `E_PHI_INT` at a *different*
object leaves no before/after pair to compare — in 3J that hid a ×3.028 change in DHW draw across
all 56 cells while every value check reported zero violations. Both arms were seen failing (`I15`,
`I16`).

`G8.16` does not trust the cell manifest's own `schedule_fold` either: the diary is located **by
content** (name + md5) among the three Step 7 bundles on disk, and the fold is read out of the
bundle's own `manifest.json`.

### 3.2 🔴 Vacuity is declared, never passed

At `f = 0` the multiplier is identically 1.0 and there is **no Step 7 diary at all**, so `G8.12` and
`G8.16` report **`NOT_EVALUABLE` on those 88 scenario-cells** rather than a pass they did not earn
(`FINDING 95`). The flat series is separately asserted to be exactly 1.0 (**`V8.h`**, PASS 88, seen
failing as `I18`), so "no schedule" cannot hide a wrong one.

## 4. What the campaign measured

### 4.1 🔴 `FINDING 133` — the annual channel is empty and the peak channel is not

Median over cells, ensemble mean per cell, at every level of `f` (the pre-registration forbids
quoting one level):

| `f` | `es` ΔEUI % | `uk` ΔEUI % | `it` ΔEUI % | `es` Δpeak % | `uk` Δpeak % | `it` Δpeak % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.15 | +0.172 | −0.052 | −0.007 | +0.859 | +0.549 | +0.439 |
| 0.30 | +0.371 | −0.094 | −0.008 | +1.805 | +1.113 | +0.893 |
| 0.50 | +0.683 | −0.132 | −0.000 | +3.016 | +1.927 | +1.622 |
| **1.00** | **+1.818** | **−0.036** | **+0.059** | **+6.384** | **+4.538** | **+3.959** |

Absolute EUI at `f = 0`: `es` 24.92, `uk` 104.47, `it` 81.00 kWh/(m²·a) — and read `FINDING 121`
before comparing any of those to TABULA.

🔴 **This corrects `FINDING 128` and does not merely extend it.** 8.4 measured +1.60 % annual and
+10.84 % peak on one archetype, one diary, on the Leg-4 pilot and the misaligned calendar. Re-run on
the corrected artefacts the same cell reads **+0.18 % annual and +7.70 % peak**, and at campaign
scale the annual median is **within ±0.06 % in two folds of three**. `FINDING 128`'s direction
stands — the channel is peak — and **its magnitudes do not**.

### 4.2 🔴 `FINDING 134` — the effect against the free parameter nobody registered

Between-diary standard deviation, median over cells, at `f = 1.00`, beside the effect:

| fold | ΔEUI % | diary sd of EUI % | ratio | Δpeak % | diary sd of peak % | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `es` | +1.818 | **1.884** | **0.97** | +6.384 | 3.735 | 1.71 |
| `uk` | −0.036 | 0.364 | 0.10 | +4.538 | 2.580 | 1.76 |
| `it` | +0.059 | 0.155 | 0.38 | +3.959 | 1.957 | 2.02 |

**On annual heating the effect is smaller than the diary spread in all three folds** — in Spain it is
the same size, in the UK and Italy an order of magnitude smaller. **On peak it is 1.7–2.0× the
spread in all three.** No annual statement of the form *"occupancy injection changes annual heating
demand by X %"* survives this table; the peak statement does, with its spread attached. This is what
the ensemble was run to find out, and a single-diary campaign would have reported the Spanish
+1.8 % as a result.

### 4.3 `FINDING 135` — the peak's hour of day never moves; its date does

| | |
|---|---|
| annual-peak **hour of day**, fold-mean profile | **7** in every fold at **every** level of `f` |
| between-diary span of that hour of day, median over cells | `es` 2 h, `uk` **0 h**, `it` **0 h** |
| between-diary span of the annual-peak **hour index**, median | `es` 2 h, `uk` 265 h, `it` **7,824 h** |
| mean **diurnal** profile peak hour, `f = 0 → 1` | `es` 6 → 6, `uk` **5 → 7**, `it` **6 → 7** (both at `f ≥ 0.50`) |

The annual peak sits at the morning thermostat recovery hour, which is a property of the setpoint
schedule and **not** of occupancy, so occupancy cannot move it — while the **date** on which the
annual peak falls moves by up to 326 days between diaries. What occupancy does move is the shape of
the mean day: the diurnal peak shifts **two hours later in the UK and one in Italy** at `f ≥ 0.50`,
and not at all in Spain. `G8.6`'s ≤ 1 h threshold is a **reproducibility** tripwire against the
re-run under `D-S8-5` item 2 and is not being compared to this.

### 4.4 The effect is ordered by dwelling class, and the ordering is the same in all three folds

Median Δpeak % at `f = 1.00`:

| class | `es` | `uk` | `it` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AB` apartment block | **+10.13** | **+8.69** | **+5.82** |
| `MFH` multi-family | +6.48 | +6.37 | +4.46 |
| `SFH` single-family | +5.30 | +3.55 | +2.21 |
| `TH` terraced | +4.29 | +3.06 | +2.51 |

Monotone in compactness in every fold, with `AB` 2.3–2.6× `TH`. The largest single cell is
`es_AB_ES06` at **+31.47 % peak / +26.94 % annual** — and its own diary spread is **14.81 %**, so it
is quoted as the top of a range and never on its own.

## 6. What was deliberately NOT done

* **No band moved, no threshold defined, no checker edited to make a gate pass.** `prereg.md`
  untouched.
* **No setback schedule was added.** `F_red_htr` stays the TABULA scalar — the provenance file's
  §9.5 says that a real setback plus an occupancy-driven gains profile makes the sweep measure two
  things at once.
* **The 8.1 IDFs were not rebuilt.** The calendar was fixed on the schedule side, which is additive;
  changing the `RunPeriod` would have invalidated 88 recorded md5s and the whole 8.3 control.
* **`G8.7` was not turned into a gate.** It is INFO permanently by `D-S8-5` item 1 (a).
* **The 8.4 record was not rewritten.** Its numbers were measured on the Leg-4 pilot and the
  misaligned calendar; the correction is recorded here and as a dated addendum there, because a
  record that is edited to agree with a later run is not a record.
