Both are deep enough to be worth acting on. Where they agree, the defect is real and goes straight into
the work. Where only one found it, the plan below re-verifies it against the repository before any edit.

**Two facts I re-measured myself before planning around them**, because a peer's numbers are evidence only
after they are re-measured:

1. `Step6_docs/outputs_step6/g67_leg5_{es,uk,it}.json` line 122-126 each carry a `steering` block:
   `passes: true`, `r2` 0.9896766532040907 / 0.9914229425317623 / 0.9940690138212029, `r2_min` 0.80,
   slope 0.2863 / 0.7144 / 0.3797. The reported 7.30 B model's steering arm **was computed**.

   string reads *"slope 0.4153 over the 5 active aggregates ... (the residual bucket AC4-8 is excluded,

   paper are therefore **the five-channel ruled definition**, not the six-channel one the paper says
   they are. `D-S6-13` at `Step6_docs/4thJ_06_transfer.md:2523-2537` sets the definition and prints the
   pilot's parallel row, so the two arms are like for like.

`FINDING 275` is refuted by the artefact it cites. The repair is a read, not a run.

🔴 Nothing in this plan moves a gate, a band, a verdict or a registered definition. `G6.7` still FAILs in
