## 1. WHAT WAS BUILT, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Every building in the layout census is emitted **twice at every `f`**: **Case A**, one diary replicated
to all `N_u` zones, and **Case B**, `N_u` independently sampled diaries — same footprint, same zone
areas, same arm, same fold, same seed stream. `delta_div = Metric(B) − Metric(A)` is formed **within
footprint** and never across buildings. `G10.20` scores the pairing; `G10.13`, `G10.8`, `G10.9` and
`G10.19` are re-scored on the paired artefact; `G10.21` is scored **`NOT_EVALUABLE` with its population
named**, because it lives on simulated building power and no Step 10 cell has been simulated.

🔴 **This is not the `H10` test and it is not a simulation.** Work item 10.9's row in the work-item
table reads `Simulation? yes` — the simulated half belongs with 10.5 / 10.6, which wait on 10.3 and
OpenUBEM `EU-04`. What 10.9 owed and could deliver today is the **paired emission and its gate**; what
it could not deliver is named in §7 below rather than left for a reader to discover.

## 4. THREE FINDINGS

### 🔴 `FINDING 158` — on the emitted `phi_int` channel the coincidence factor is **exactly 1 by construction**, for every `N_u` and every `f`

**1,450 of 1,450** Case B cells with `N_u ≥ 2` return `CF_phi = 1` to a bound of 1e-12, and **1,450 of
1,450** contain at least one hour at which **every zone is simultaneously at its own maximum** —
**minimum 396 such hours** in the worst cell of the whole census. The mechanism is not subtle: every
Step 7 presence series reaches **1.0**, so independently drawn households still share hours at which all
of them are in.

**Consequences, and they are not cosmetic.**

* **Diversity cannot lower the driver peak.** Any `CF < 1` that Step 10 reports must come from the
  **thermal response**, not from the schedules. `G10.21` therefore **cannot be discharged by any
  pre-simulation artefact** — which is why it is scored `NOT_EVALUABLE` here rather than reported green
  on a number that exists.
* 🔴 **A pre-registered risk for 10.6, recorded before it runs.** §1.1a chose `CF` because *"in the
  synchronised case it is exactly 1 by construction — the comparison is against a constant, not against
  a spread"*. That is still true. What this finding adds is that the **constant is not evidence**: on
  this artefact the independent case is also exactly 1, so `CF ≈ 1` on both arms is a possible Step 10
  outcome that would mean *the metric has no dynamic range here*, not *`H10₀` holds*. If 10.6 returns
  `CF_A = CF_B = 1.000` on simulated power, that is a **`NOT_EVALUABLE`, not a null**, and the
  distinction has to be made before the numbers arrive, not after.
* **It felled a guard of ours.** See §5.

⚪ `H10`'s text is **not** edited, `G10.21`'s threshold is **not** moved, and no gate was loosened.

### 🔴 `FINDING 159` — the 99th-percentile hourly power **equals the peak** in 2,970 of 2,970 cells

`G10.21`(i) asks for `CF` **and** the 99th-percentile hourly power. On the driver channel the two are
the same number: presence saturates at 1.0 for far more than 1 % of the year, so `q99 = max` exactly —
**2,970 of 2,970 cells**, and **2,376 of 2,376** among the `f > 0` cells alone. Every one of the 1,485
delta rows therefore carries `delta_div_p99 == delta_div_peak` to 1e-9.

**The percentile is a second channel only after simulation.** Before it, quoting both is quoting one
number twice — the `FINDING 154` shape (a statistic that looks like a second opinion and is not).

### 🔴 `FINDING 160` — `delta_div` on the driver channel is a statement about **which diary Case A replicates**, not about diversity

Case A replicates unit 0's draw, and **which** unit that is, is a free parameter. With all zones sharing
one series the building peak is `3.0 · ΣA_z · max(m_u)`, so the `N_u` alternative Case A's are computable
from Case B's own zone series at zero cost. Measured at `f = 1.00`: the median `delta_div` is
**0.73 %** (Arm D) and **1.22 %** (Arm F) against a Case-A choice spread of **31.77 %** and **30.00 %** —
ratios **0.198** and **0.295**, and **0 of 290 rows** in either arm have an effect larger than the
spread of the parameter that was free.

**This is `FINDING 143`'s shape, caught before it could be published rather than after.** The ratio is
printed in `delta_div_within_footprint.csv` as its own column, `abs_delta_over_choice_spread`, so no
reader has to reconstruct it.

## 5. THE BATTERY, AND THE GUARD IT FELLED

🔴 **The first `W10.9` scored `G10.21`(ii) literally — "Case A's `CF_phi` must be 1" — and the battery's
own `case_a_independent` case, which is exactly the harness defect that clause exists to catch, came
back `PASS`.** `CF_phi` is 1 for the independent case too (`FINDING 158`). A guard whose discriminator
is **constant in the ground truth** is not a guard; it is failure class #7 in this project's own
catalogue, and it was found by running the battery rather than by reading the code.

**The fix is additive and it moved no threshold.** `W10.9`'s verdict is now taken on **series
identity** — the `sha256` of each emitted file, taken before reduction, must be the same across a Case A
cell's `N_u` zones. The `CF = 1` arm is retained and reported and explicitly marked
**`CARRIED, NOT SCORED`** in the artefact itself, so it can never be quoted as evidence that the
synchronised case was built correctly. `W10.12` was added to publish the degeneracy as a measurement.

## 6. DECISIONS THIS ITEM HAD TO TAKE

1. 🔴 **Arm F carries a Case B, and its zones are STOREYS.** Two documents pull: 10.4 ruled *"Arm F gets
   one diary for the whole building"*, §6.6 says *"every Step 10 building therefore runs twice"* and
   that the pairing *"makes `H10` testable on Arm F geometry"*. Read as: **10.4 governs the production
   assignment** (untouched — `W10.11` proves the Arm D half reproduces it row for row), **10.9 is a
   paired probe**. On Arm F the probe measures diversity across **stacked storey zones**, which is the
   magnitude of the very averaging bias `G10.22` labels as a lower bound — measured instead of asserted.
   Every row carries `zone_semantics` = `dwelling` / `storey`; `G10.19` counts **Arm D only**; `G10.9`
   keeps the arms out of one statistic. **An Arm F delta is never an `H10` dwelling result.**
   ⚪ Flagged for the author: if this reading is wrong, the Arm F half of the delta table is what has to
   be withdrawn, and nothing else in the item depends on it.
2. **Case A replicates Case B's unit-0 draw**, so the pair differs in exactly one thing and the seed
   stream is literally the same — `G10.20`'s "same seed policy" clause. The cost of that choice is
   `FINDING 160`, and the choice spread is reported beside every row rather than being left implicit.
3. **`G10.13` is scored per case.** Keying the per-building area-weighted arm by `building_id` alone
   would weight Case A's zones together with Case B's and could hide a zone failure inside a pooled
   building mean.
4. **`CF_phi`, never `CF`.** Every artefact carries a `cf_basis` field reading
   `phi_int_gain_channel_NOT_simulated_power`. Heating demand peaks when gains are **low**; quoting the
   gain-channel number as `CF` would be a basis change wearing a result's clothes.
5. **A null case is in the battery.** A battery in which every case fires proves the cases fire; it does
   not prove the gates are quiet when nothing is wrong.
