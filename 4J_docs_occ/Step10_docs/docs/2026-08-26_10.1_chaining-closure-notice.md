## 1. Artefact 1 of 4 — the frozen rule text

**The adopted convention is `independent`, seed 1.** Stated so it can be re-implemented from this
paragraph alone:

> Each person's year is assembled by drawing, **independently for every calendar day**, one diary
> day from the pool of diary days matching that person's stratum and that day's day type. There is
> no persistence between consecutive days and no day is held and repeated. The stratum key is
> `(strat_age_band, strat_sex, strat_hh_type, strat_econ_status, strat_day_type)`, matched at full
> depth and **backed off one field at a time** when a bucket is empty; `strat_day_type` is **never**
> dropped, so a Saturday diary is never served on a Tuesday. The household series is the fraction
> `(members present) / (household size)` per timestep, in `[0, 1]`, so the head count stays in
> `People.Number of People` and never in the schedule.

Formally this is `rho = 0` on the pre-registered persistence axis
`RULE_POINTS = (independent 0.0, habit 0.25, habit 0.50, habit 0.75, habit 0.90, static 1.0)`.
The emitter's selftest asserts that `rho = 0` **is** `independent` and `rho = 1` **is** `static`
under the same seed and draw order, which is what makes this one axis sampled at six points rather
than six unrelated rules.

**Emission parameters, as recorded in every shipped bundle manifest:**

| field | value |
|---|---|
| `rule` | `independent` |
| `rho` | `0.0` |
| `seed` | `1` |
| `year` | `2017` (Sunday start, matching the IDF `RunPeriod`) |
| `timestep_min` | `60` |
| `n_values_per_schedule_expected` | `8760` |
| `interpolate_to_timestep` | `No` |
| `diary_origin_hour` | `4` |
| `rotated_to_midnight` | `true` |

🔴 **`diary_origin_hour = 4` with `rotated_to_midnight = true` is the day-boundary convention, and
it must be read together.** HETUS diaries begin at **04:00**, not midnight. Every schedule shipped
before 2026-08-26 applied occupancy **four hours early**; `D-S9-3` ruled the fix and every campaign
was re-emitted on rotated schedules and re-run (`FINDING 141`). A consumer that re-derives these
series from raw diaries and does **not** rotate will disagree with the shipped bundles by four
hours.

## 2. Artefact 2 of 4 — the seed policy

Two seeds are in play and they are not the same thing.

| | value | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| **production seed** | **1** | the single seed of the adopted convention. Every published Step 8 and Step 9 dataset is emitted at `independent`, seed 1 |
| **experiment seeds** | **11, 22, 33, 44, 55** | five seeds per rule point, used **only** to measure the convention's sensitivity. Not a product |

🔴 **The five experiment seeds are a pre-registered minimum, not a convenience.** The runner
**refuses** fewer than five:

> *"A single realisation per rule is a curve with no error bar, therefore no way to be wrong,
> therefore no way to fail."*

⚪ **Why a single production seed is defensible here, and only here:** the measurement in §4 shows
the spread across seeds within one rule is **larger** than the spread across rules. A campaign that
is insensitive to the rule at a level below its own seed noise cannot be made more correct by
averaging seeds — it can only be made more expensive. This is stated as a consequence of the
measurement, not as a general principle.

**The production seed is checkable in the artefact, not just in prose.** The injected campaign
manifest names its bundles:

```
"schedule_bundles": {"es": "leg5_es_independent_seed1",
                     "uk": "leg5_uk_independent_seed1",
                     "it": "leg5_it_independent_seed1"}
```

Every ratio in the table is **below 1**, in every fold, on every metric that varies at all. No
chaining rule can be justified over another on the quantity the trigger is defined on, and the
convention was chosen for simplicity, not for performance.

### 4.3 🔴 One comparison in `FINDING 136` is WITHDRAWN and must not be quoted

The pre-rotation record said the occupancy effect was **17–60×** the chaining convention's whole

🟢 **This does not weaken decision 14.** Both quantities are below the between-diary spread in `uk`,

approached in any fold**. But the `17–60×` sentence is superseded and any OpenUBEM artefact
repeating it should drop it.

## 6. What this notice does NOT do

* **It does not move `G7.18`'s trigger.** 25 % is quoted as registered and is not re-derived.
* **It does not claim the chaining rule is unimportant in general.** It is unimportant **for the
  quantities this paper reports, on these three archetypes, at the `f` where the schedule matters
  most**. A metric this campaign does not report — activity vocabulary — is the one axis on which
  the rules *do* separate decisively (`FINDING 96`: ratios 18.17 / 11.89 / 18.36), and it is also
  the one axis with no empirical reference anywhere in this project.
* **It does not generalise beyond three archetypes.** One per fold is the registered sizing, and all
  three are `AB` — the class where the occupancy peak effect was **largest**, so if any class were
  going to show a chaining sensitivity it would be this one.
* **It does not request anything of OpenUBEM.** No re-run, no pipeline change, no parameter to
  adopt on the OpenUBEM side beyond reading `independent`, seed 1 as the standing convention.
