# RL16. The longitudinal axis: can a fine-tuned model be pushed to a future year, and what would make that defensible?

## Section A. Direct answer

Direct extrapolative forecasting of time-use diaries to a future year (such as 2030) using the internal weights of a fine-tuned large language model trained on two or three sparse European Harmonised European Time Use Surveys (HETUS) waves is strictly indefensible and must be abandoned. Across European countries, HETUS provides at most two harmonised microdata rounds (Round 1 around 2000 and Round 2 around 2010), while Round 3 (2018 to 2026) remains embargoed until at least 2027 with fieldwork severely fractured by the COVID-19 pandemic. Fitting an autoregressive temporal trend across two discrete points spaced a decade apart conflates methodological breaks (such as Activity Coding List revisions and diary mode shifts) with genuine behavioural change. The methodologically rigorous and defensible longitudinal design for Paper 4 is a two-pillar architecture: first, formal Kitagawa-Oaxaca-Blinder shift-share decomposition of observed historical between-wave changes into demographic-compositional versus behavioural components; second, exogenous counterfactual scenario projection, where the fine-tuned model generates conditional diary distributions conditioned on official demographic, labour, and telework projections published by Eurostat, Cedefop, and the European Commission.

* **Adoption of Kitagawa-Oaxaca-Blinder decomposition (Section B, Rows 10, 11):** The results section will present a formal demographic decomposition: "Observed changes in aggregate diurnal presence and domestic activity budgets between HETUS Round 1 and Round 2 are decomposed using the Kitagawa-Oaxaca-Blinder framework into compositional effects attributable to population ageing, household shrinking, and female labour force participation, versus genuine within-stratum behavioural shifts."
* **Exogenous counterfactual scenario projection framing (Section B, Rows 15, 16, 18, 19):** The 2030 forecasting claims must be positioned as conditional policy and demographic scenario projections rather than autonomous model predictions: "Future 2030 occupancy schedules are generated by conditioning our invariant conditional diary distribution model on synthetic populations synthesized from official Eurostat EUROPOP2023 baseline demographic projections, Cedefop employment outlooks, and EU-LFS teleworking penetration bands."

## Section G. Contradictions, gaps, open questions, and your own negative controls

### Contradictions and Gaps Identified
* **HETUS Microdata Availability vs Tabulations:** Public descriptions frequently refer to "three HETUS waves" (2000, 2010, 2020). In reality, Eurostat provides centralized Scientific Use File (SUF) microdata solely for Round 2 (2010). Round 1 microdata was never harmonized into a uniform Eurostat SUF release (it exists primarily as aggregated tables or through the separate Multinational Time Use Study archive at Oxford/CeTI), and Round 3 microdata will not be released by Eurostat until 2027 or later. Any research claiming to train an LLM directly on 3 harmonized Eurostat microdata waves is factually impossible today.
* **Eurostat Household Projections Gap:** Eurostat publishes comprehensive official population projections by age and sex (`proj_23np`), but has discontinued regular production of harmonized EU-wide household projections. To generate future household compositions for UBEM synthetic populations, researchers must either apply headship rate models to EUROPOP2023 or ingest country-specific household projections from national statistical bodies (e.g. Insee in France, Destatis in Germany, ONS in the UK).
* **Mode Effects vs Real Behavioural Trends in Round 3:** Preliminary reports from Round 3 national surveys indicate substantial mode effects from introducing smartphone apps and web diaries (CAWI). App diaries capture more frequent, shorter activity fragments and show lower compliance for secondary activity reporting compared to traditional paper booklets. When Round 3 microdata eventually arrives, naively comparing 2010 paper diaries to 2020 app diaries will misclassify measurement artifact as behavioural change.

### COVID-19 Fieldwork Status Per Country (Round 3)
* **Pre-Pandemic Completion:** Very few participating countries completed HETUS Round 3 fieldwork prior to March 2020.
* **Fieldwork Conducted During Acute Restrictions (2020-2021):** Finland conducted fieldwork from September 2020 to September 2021; the UK Office for National Statistics ran online time-use diary waves across 2020 and 2021; Austria fielded from 2021 to 2022. Diaries from these periods exhibit artificially suppressed travel, mandatory telework, and school-closure child care loads.
* **Fieldwork Postponed to Post-Acute Period (2022-2025):** Germany postponed its ZVE survey to 2022; France and Spain rescheduled national time-use collection to 2024-2025.
* **Verdict on Pandemic Handling:** Data collected during national lockdown mandates must be excluded from baseline residential occupancy calibration or isolated as an explicit stress-test regime. Long-term forecasting cannot treat mid-lockdown occupancy as an intermediate trend anchor.

### Forecast Defensibility Verdict
* **Direct LLM Weight Extrapolation: REJECTED (Indefensible).** An LLM cannot extrapolate temporal dynamics from 2 sparse time points. Prompting with `<YEAR_2030>` is meaningless out-of-distribution generation.
* **Counterfactual Exogenous Scenario Projection: ACCEPTED (Defensible).** Conditioning the generative model on demographic marginals, labour force participation, and telework status allows future occupancy schedules to be rigorously generated by driving the input conditioning vector with official EUROPOP2023, Cedefop, and EU-LFS projections.

### Negative Controls and Sanity Checks
* **Temporal Permutation Control:** When evaluating whether a model has learned meaningful temporal structure between Wave 1 and Wave 2, randomly shuffling the year conditioning tokens during training must result in zero statistically significant shift in generated activity budgets. If an unconditioned or shuffled model performs equally well, the model has memorized demographic correlations rather than temporal shifts.
* **Invariant Biological Activity Baseline:** Biological routines (such as nocturnal sleep duration and personal hygiene) exhibit high secular stability across decades (typically varying by less than 10-15 minutes over a 20-year span). An LLM projection to 2030 that shifts mean sleep duration by >45 minutes or moves the night-time sleep window into midday fails the negative control and indicates distributional collapse.
* **Counterfactual Sensitivity Gate:** Shifting the conditioning attribute from `Work_From_Home = False` to `Work_From_Home = True` must generate a statistically significant increase in daytime at-home presence (between 09:00 and 17:00) of at least 180 minutes, accompanied by the collapse of commute travel episodes. If the generated diary is insensitive to this flag, the model is failing to condition on the primary energy-relevant lever.
