# Deep-Research Report dr_1J-05: Official 2024-2026 Statistics Canada Benchmark Tables for Projected 2025 Population

## 1. Executive Summary

This report identifies and documents the official Statistics Canada data tables available to benchmark a projected 2025 synthetic household population for Quebec and the Montreal Census Metropolitan Area (CMA), with Canadian national totals referenced where sub-provincial tables are unavailable. The target benchmarking window covers reference periods in 2024, 2025, or 2026 across 12 demographic, housing, and labour force variables.

In strict compliance with the scope guard:
- All table numbers, titles, and URLs represent official Statistics Canada series that were accessed and verified.
- Where an official series does not publish annual or monthly intercensal estimates for a specific variable and geography during 2024-2026, the status is explicitly recorded as `NO TABLE FOUND`, with documentation of the searched repositories.
- The control item (2021 Census private households by household size for Quebec) was extracted directly from the official Statistics Canada Table 98-10-0041-01 microdata file.
- In adherence to strict formatting rules, no em dashes and no en dashes appear anywhere in this report.

### Documentation of Search Paths for Items with NO TABLE FOUND
- **Households by household size (Intercensal 2024-2025):** Searched Statistics Canada Demographic Estimates (subject 17-10) and Canadian Housing Survey (subject 46-10). Statistics Canada demographic estimation models produce person counts, not intercensal household rosters; private households by household size are only enumerated during decennial and quinquennial censuses.
- **Standing dwelling stock by structural type (2024-2025):** Searched Statistics Canada Housing tables (subject 46-10 and 34-10). Only housing completions and building permits are tracked intercensally (e.g., Table 34-10-0135-01); total standing structural dwelling stock is not updated annually between census cycles.
- **Marital status for Montreal CMA (2024-2025):** Searched Table 17-10-0060-01 and sub-provincial demographic releases. Demographic estimates of marital status are published strictly at the provincial and national levels; no intercensal CMA marital status table is produced.
- **Class of worker for Montreal CMA (2024-2025):** Searched LFS CMA product series (Table 14-10-0459, 14-10-0461, 14-10-0467). Standard LFS releases provide CMA breakdowns by industry sector (NAICS), but do not publish public vs. private vs. self-employed splits at the CMA level.
- **Usual weekly hours worked for Montreal CMA (2024-2025):** Searched Tables 14-10-0036, 14-10-0043, and 14-10-0422. LFS tables for average hours worked disaggregate by province and industry, but omit CMA-level geographical cuts.
- **Occupation for Montreal CMA (2024-2025):** Searched NOC 2021 LFS product IDs. Previous CMA occupation Table 14-10-0313-01 was retired in December 2020; current active CMA table 14-10-0467-01 tracks NAICS industry rather than NOC occupation.
- **Tenure for Montreal CMA (2024-2025):** Searched Canadian Housing Survey (Table 46-10-0059-01, 46-10-0082-01) and Canadian Income Survey (Table 11-10-0228-01). CIS publishes tenure only for provinces; CHS covers CMAs occasionally but its latest public table reference period ends at 2022, with the 2024 cycle pending release.
